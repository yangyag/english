# 필수 영어단어 401–450

## 401. arrive

- 뜻: 도착하다
- 예문: The train will arrive at the station in ten minutes.

## 402. north

- 뜻: 북쪽, 북쪽의
- 예문: They drove north for two hours to reach the mountains.

## 403. fill

- 뜻: 채우다
- 예문: Please fill this bottle with cold water from the fridge.

## 404. note

- 뜻: 메모, 쪽지
- 예문: She left a note on the table for her sister.

## 405. card

- 뜻: 카드
- 예문: He paid for dinner with his credit card last night.

## 406. finish

- 뜻: 끝내다, 마치다
- 예문: I need to finish my homework before dinner tonight.

## 407. ice

- 뜻: 얼음
- 예문: She put some ice in her glass of lemonade.

## 408. dark

- 뜻: 어두운, 어둠
- 예문: It was too dark to see the path without a light.

## 409. smile

- 뜻: 미소; 미소 짓다
- 예문: She gave me a warm smile when I entered the room.

## 410. bag

- 뜻: 가방, 봉지
- 예문: He packed his clothes in a small bag for the trip.

## 411. cook

- 뜻: 요리하다; 요리사
- 예문: My father likes to cook pasta for the family on Sundays.

## 412. exercise

- 뜻: 운동; 연습하다
- 예문: I try to exercise for thirty minutes after work each day.

## 413. boy

- 뜻: 소년, 남자아이
- 예문: The boy kicked the ball across the grass in the park.

## 414. introduce

- 뜻: 소개하다
- 예문: Let me introduce you to my friend from high school.

## 415. near

- 뜻: 가까운, 가까이에
- 예문: There is a small bakery near our house on Main Street.

## 416. earth

- 뜻: 지구, 흙
- 예문: The earth goes around the sun once every year.

## 417. blue

- 뜻: 푸른, 파란색의
- 예문: She wore a blue dress to the party last Saturday.

## 418. fish

- 뜻: 물고기; 낚시하다
- 예문: We caught a big fish in the lake this morning.

## 419. oil

- 뜻: 기름, 석유
- 예문: Add a little oil to the pan before you cook the eggs.

## 420. shape

- 뜻: 모양, 형태
- 예문: The cookies were in the shape of a small star.

## 421. box

- 뜻: 상자
- 예문: Please put those old books into the box by the door.

## 422. sugar

- 뜻: 설탕
- 예문: Would you like some sugar in your coffee this morning?

## 423. season

- 뜻: 계절, 시즌
- 예문: Spring is my favorite season because the flowers start to bloom.

## 424. sun

- 뜻: 태양, 해
- 예문: The sun was shining brightly over the quiet beach this morning.

## 425. surprise

- 뜻: 놀람; 놀라게 하다
- 예문: They planned a surprise party for their teacher after school.

## 426. park

- 뜻: 공원; 주차하다
- 예문: We had a picnic in the park on a sunny afternoon.

## 427. front

- 뜻: 앞, 앞쪽
- 예문: Please wait for me in front of the library after class.

## 428. quick

- 뜻: 빠른, 신속한
- 예문: We need a quick lunch because the meeting starts soon.

## 429. gas

- 뜻: 가스, 휘발유
- 예문: We stopped to buy gas before the long drive home.

## 430. train

- 뜻: 기차; 훈련하다
- 예문: I take the train to work every morning at seven.

## 431. key

- 뜻: 열쇠; 핵심
- 예문: I cannot find the key to the front door of my house.

## 432. dead

- 뜻: 죽은
- 예문: The plant was dead because nobody had watered it for weeks.

## 433. hide

- 뜻: 숨다, 숨기다
- 예문: The children like to hide behind the sofa during the game.

## 434. farm

- 뜻: 농장
- 예문: They grow vegetables and keep chickens on their small farm.

## 435. kitchen

- 뜻: 부엌, 주방
- 예문: She was cooking dinner in the kitchen when I came home.

## 436. west

- 뜻: 서쪽, 서쪽의
- 예문: The sun sets in the west every evening over the hills.

## 437. cry

- 뜻: 울다, 외치다
- 예문: The baby started to cry when her mother left the room.

## 438. clean

- 뜻: 깨끗한; 청소하다
- 예문: Please clean your room before your friends come over today.

## 439. dress

- 뜻: 드레스, 옷; 옷을 입다
- 예문: She bought a new dress for her sister's wedding next month.

## 440. touch

- 뜻: 만지다, 닿다
- 예문: Please do not touch the paintings hanging on the museum walls.

## 441. strange

- 뜻: 이상한, 낯선
- 예문: I heard a strange noise coming from the empty house.

## 442. marry

- 뜻: 결혼하다
- 예문: They plan to marry next spring in a small garden.

## 443. finger

- 뜻: 손가락
- 예문: He cut his finger while he was chopping vegetables for dinner.

## 444. station

- 뜻: 역, 정거장
- 예문: We will meet at the train station at six o'clock.

## 445. hello

- 뜻: 안녕, 안녕하세요
- 예문: She always says hello to her neighbors in the morning.

## 446. fair

- 뜻: 공정한, 공평한
- 예문: The teacher tried to be fair to every student in class.

## 447. danger

- 뜻: 위험
- 예문: Swimming too far from the shore can put you in danger.

## 448. shall

- 뜻: ~할까요, ~할 것이다
- 예문: Shall we go for a walk after dinner tonight?

## 449. tall

- 뜻: 키가 큰, 높은
- 예문: My brother is tall enough to reach the top shelf.

## 450. island

- 뜻: 섬
- 예문: They spent a quiet week on a small island near the coast.
