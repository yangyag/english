# 필수 영어단어 201–250

## 201. car

- 뜻: 자동차
- 예문: She parked her car in front of the small shop.

## 202. age

- 뜻: 나이, 시대
- 예문: At his age, he still plays soccer every weekend.

## 203. must

- 뜻: ~해야 한다, 반드시
- 예문: You must wear a helmet when you ride a bike.

## 204. far

- 뜻: 멀리, 먼
- 예문: The train station is not far from our house.

## 205. face

- 뜻: 얼굴, 직면하다
- 예문: She washed her face and looked in the mirror.

## 206. enough

- 뜻: 충분한, 충분히
- 예문: We do not have enough time to finish the report.

## 207. lead

- 뜻: 이끌다, 선두
- 예문: She will lead the group on the mountain hike.

## 208. music

- 뜻: 음악
- 예문: Soft music helped him relax after a long day.

## 209. join

- 뜻: 가입하다, 합류하다
- 예문: He wants to join the school soccer club this year.

## 210. until

- 뜻: ~까지
- 예문: Please wait here until I come back with the keys.

## 211. eat

- 뜻: 먹다
- 예문: We usually eat dinner together at seven o'clock.

## 212. eye

- 뜻: 눈

## 213. lose

- 뜻: 잃다, 지다
- 예문: Do not lose your ticket before you board the train.

## 214. win

- 뜻: 이기다, 얻다
- 예문: Our school team hopes to win the final game.

## 215. hard

- 뜻: 단단한, 어려운, 열심히

## 216. side

- 뜻: 쪽, 옆, 측면

## 217. student

## 218. team

- 뜻: 팀

## 219. true

- 뜻: 진실한, 진짜의
- 예문: Is it true that the store will close next month?

## 220. under

- 뜻: ~아래에
- 예문: The cat is sleeping under the warm kitchen table.

## 221. strong

- 뜻: 강한, 튼튼한

## 222. teach

- 뜻: 가르치다

## 223. listen

- 뜻: 듣다, 귀를 기울이다

## 224. set

- 뜻: 놓다, 정하다, 세트
- 예문: Please set the plates on the table before dinner.

## 225. night

- 뜻: 밤
- 예문: We stayed up late talking on Saturday night.

## 226. begin

- 뜻: 시작하다
- 예문: Classes begin at nine in the morning every day.

## 227. walk

- 뜻: 걷다, 산책
- 예문: They walk to school together when the weather is nice.

## 228. watch

- 뜻: 보다, 시계

## 229. wear

- 뜻: 입다, 착용하다
- 예문: You should wear a coat because it is cold outside.

## 230. pass

- 뜻: 지나가다, 합격하다, 건네다
- 예문: Please pass me the salt when you have a moment.

## 231. month

- 뜻: 달, 개월
- 예문: She will visit her grandparents next month for a week.

## 232. sit

- 뜻: 앉다
- 예문: Please sit down and wait for the doctor to come.

## 233. soon

- 뜻: 곧, 머지않아
- 예문: Dinner will be ready soon, so please wash your hands.

## 234. build

- 뜻: 짓다, 만들다
- 예문: They plan to build a new library near the park.

## 235. hope

- 뜻: 희망, 바라다
- 예문: I hope you feel better after a good night of sleep.

## 236. space

- 뜻: 공간, 우주
- 예문: There is not enough space for another chair here.

## 237. easy

- 뜻: 쉬운

## 238. break

## 250. father
