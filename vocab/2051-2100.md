# 필수 영어단어 2051–2100

## 2051. lung

- 뜻: 폐, 허파
- 예문: Smoking for years had already damaged his left lung badly.

## 2052. founder

- 뜻: 설립자, 창립자
- 예문: The founder of the company still visits the office every week.

## 2053. intelligent

- 뜻: 똑똑한, 지능이 높은
- 예문: She asked an intelligent question during the science lecture.

## 2054. mechanism

- 뜻: 장치, 메커니즘, 작동 원리
- 예문: This locking mechanism keeps the door shut during strong winds.

## 2055. otherwise

- 뜻: 그렇지 않으면, 달리
- 예문: Wear a coat, otherwise you will get cold on the walk.

## 2056. virtual

- 뜻: 가상의, 사실상의
- 예문: The class met in a virtual classroom instead of the school.

## 2057. declare

- 뜻: 선언하다, 신고하다
- 예문: They will declare the winner after the final votes are counted.

## 2058. domestic

- 뜻: 국내의, 가정의
- 예문: Domestic flights are cheaper than trips to other countries.

## 2059. artificial

- 뜻: 인공의, 가짜의
- 예문: The lake is surrounded by artificial grass and plastic flowers.

## 2060. additional

- 뜻: 추가의, 부가적인
- 예문: We need additional chairs for the guests arriving this evening.

## 2061. ensure

- 뜻: 보장하다, 확실히 하다
- 예문: Please ensure that all windows are closed before you leave.

## 2062. testing

- 뜻: 검사, 시험, 테스트
- 예문: The new software is still in testing before the public release.

## 2063. path

- 뜻: 길, 오솔길, 경로
- 예문: A narrow path leads through the woods to the old farm.

## 2064. rid

- 뜻: 제거하다, 없애다
- 예문: She finally got rid of the old clothes in her closet.

## 2065. educational

- 뜻: 교육의, 교육적인
- 예문: The museum offers educational programs for children every Saturday.

## 2066. differently

- 뜻: 다르게, 달리
- 예문: Two teachers explained the same math problem quite differently today.

## 2067. enemy

- 뜻: 적, 원수
- 예문: In the story, the hero finally faces his greatest enemy.

## 2068. peer

- 뜻: 또래, 동료; 자세히 보다
- 예문: She asked a peer in class to check her essay draft.

## 2069. enhance

- 뜻: 향상시키다, 강화하다
- 예문: Regular practice will enhance your ability to speak English clearly.

## 2070. southern

- 뜻: 남쪽의
- 예문: They spent the winter on a quiet southern beach with friends.

## 2071. magic

- 뜻: 마법, 마술
- 예문: The children still believe in magic after watching the stage show.

## 2072. youth

- 뜻: 젊음, 청소년
- 예문: The youth club meets after school to play basketball together.

## 2073. fee

- 뜻: 요금, 수수료
- 예문: There is a small fee to enter the museum on weekends.

## 2074. disorder

- 뜻: 장애, 혼란, 무질서
- 예문: The doctor explained how the sleep disorder was affecting her work.

## 2075. recall

- 뜻: 기억하다, 회수하다
- 예문: I cannot recall the name of the restaurant we visited.

## 2076. harm

- 뜻: 해, 피해; 해치다
- 예문: Too much sun can harm your skin if you stay outside.

## 2077. helpful

- 뜻: 도움이 되는, 유용한
- 예문: The clerk was very helpful when I could not find my size.

## 2078. uncomfortable

- 뜻: 불편한, 어색한
- 예문: This wooden chair is uncomfortable after sitting for a long time.

## 2079. largely

- 뜻: 주로, 대부분
- 예문: The delay was largely caused by heavy rain on the highway.

## 2080. gesture

- 뜻: 몸짓, 제스처
- 예문: He made a small gesture toward the empty chair beside him.

## 2081. trade

- 뜻: 무역, 거래; 거래하다
- 예문: The two countries increased trade after signing a new agreement.

## 2082. shame

- 뜻: 부끄러움, 수치, 유감
- 예문: She felt shame after shouting at her little brother in public.

## 2083. confuse

- 뜻: 혼란스럽게 하다, 혼동하다
- 예문: Similar street names often confuse visitors walking around the city.

## 2084. lonely

- 뜻: 외로운, 쓸쓸한
- 예문: He felt lonely after moving to a new city without friends.

## 2085. highlight

- 뜻: 강조하다, 하이라이트
- 예문: Please highlight the key dates on the calendar before the meeting.

## 2086. beer

- 뜻: 맥주
- 예문: They ordered a cold beer to share after a long hike.

## 2087. classroom

- 뜻: 교실
- 예문: The classroom was quiet while students finished their history exam.

## 2088. frequency

- 뜻: 빈도, 주파수
- 예문: The frequency of his visits increased after his mother became ill.

## 2089. chest

- 뜻: 가슴, 상자
- 예문: He kept old letters in a wooden chest under the bed.

## 2090. cooking

- 뜻: 요리, 조리
- 예문: Cooking dinner together became their favorite part of the week.

## 2091. resistance

- 뜻: 저항, 저항력
- 예문: The plan met strong resistance from neighbors living near the park.

## 2092. application

- 뜻: 신청, 지원서; 응용, 앱
- 예문: She sent her job application before the Friday afternoon deadline.

## 2093. multiple

- 뜻: 다수의, 여러 개의
- 예문: The recipe failed for multiple reasons, including too much salt.

## 2094. impress

- 뜻: 깊은 인상을 주다
- 예문: She hoped her speech would impress the judges at the contest.

## 2095. employ

- 뜻: 고용하다, 사용하다
- 예문: The factory will employ twenty new workers starting next month.

## 2096. contribution

- 뜻: 기여, 기부
- 예문: Her contribution to the project made the launch much smoother.

## 2097. estate

- 뜻: 부동산, 재산, 단지
- 예문: They sold the family estate after their grandparents passed away.

## 2098. sandwich

- 뜻: 샌드위치
- 예문: I packed a cheese sandwich and an apple for lunch today.

## 2099. willing

- 뜻: 기꺼이 하는, 자발적인
- 예문: She is willing to stay late if the team still needs help.

## 2100. bomb

- 뜻: 폭탄; 폭격하다
- 예문: The museum exhibit showed how a bomb changed life in the city.
