# 필수 영어단어 5551–5600

## 5551. concise

- 뜻: 간결한, 간단명료한
- 예문: Please keep your report concise so everyone can finish it quickly.

## 5552. vomit

- 뜻: 토하다, 구토하다
- 예문: The sick child began to vomit after eating the spoiled food.

## 5553. usefully

- 뜻: 유용하게, 쓸모 있게
- 예문: She usefully organized the files before the important meeting started.

## 5554. temper

- 뜻: 성질, 기질; 누그러뜨리다
- 예문: He lost his temper when the computer crashed during the presentation.

## 5555. speculative

- 뜻: 추측의, 투기적인
- 예문: The article offered only speculative claims about the company's future plans.

## 5556. spaceship

- 뜻: 우주선
- 예문: The spaceship slowly approached the distant planet after a long journey.

## 5557. skim

- 뜻: 훑어읽다, 스치듯 지나가다; 걷어내다
- 예문: I skim the headlines every morning before I leave for work.

## 5558. vintage

- 뜻: 빈티지의, 고전적인; 수확 연도
- 예문: She bought a vintage dress from the small shop downtown.

## 5559. wed

- 뜻: 결혼하다
- 예문: They will wed next spring in a quiet garden ceremony.

## 5560. iceberg

- 뜻: 빙산
- 예문: The ship changed course after spotting a huge iceberg ahead.

## 5561. self-control

- 뜻: 자제력, 자기 통제
- 예문: You need more self-control when you feel angry during an argument.

## 5562. stepmother

- 뜻: 계모, 새어머니
- 예문: My stepmother taught me how to bake bread on Sundays.

## 5563. kite

- 뜻: 연
- 예문: The children flew a bright kite on the windy beach.

## 5564. incline

- 뜻: 기울다, ~하는 경향이 있다; 경사
- 예문: I incline to agree with her suggestion about the new schedule.

## 5565. exceptionally

- 뜻: 예외적으로, 대단히
- 예문: The weather has been exceptionally warm for this time of year.

## 5566. overthrow

- 뜻: 전복하다, 타도하다
- 예문: Rebels tried to overthrow the government after months of unrest.

## 5567. subside

- 뜻: 가라앉다, 진정되다
- 예문: The heavy rain began to subside late in the afternoon.

## 5568. conquest

- 뜻: 정복, 점령
- 예문: The army's conquest of the city changed the region's history.

## 5569. void

- 뜻: 공허한, 무효의
- 예문: The judge declared the agreement void after finding serious fraud.

## 5570. dwell

- 뜻: 거주하다; (생각에) 머물다
- 예문: Many families still dwell in small villages along the river.

## 5571. lunar

- 뜻: 달의
- 예문: Scientists studied the lunar surface using photographs from the mission.

## 5572. moisture

- 뜻: 습기, 수분
- 예문: Plants need moisture in the soil to grow strong roots.

## 5573. penetrate

- 뜻: 관통하다, 침투하다
- 예문: The cold wind seemed to penetrate every layer of my coat.

## 5574. nourish

- 뜻: 영양을 주다, 기르다
- 예문: Fresh fruit and vegetables nourish the body and support good health.

## 5575. Possessor

- 뜻: 소유자
- 예문: The possessor of the antique watch never let anyone touch it.

## 5576. unnatural

- 뜻: 부자연스러운, 이상한
- 예문: His smile looked unnatural and made everyone in the room uneasy.

## 5577. continental

- 뜻: 대륙의
- 예문: They took a long continental train trip across several countries.

## 5578. scruple

- 뜻: 양심의 가책, 거리낌
- 예문: She felt a scruple about taking credit for another person's work.

## 5579. scenery

- 뜻: 풍경, 경치
- 예문: We stopped the car to enjoy the mountain scenery at sunset.

## 5580. shuffle

- 뜻: 뒤섞다, 발을 끌며 걷다
- 예문: Please shuffle the cards before you deal them to the players.

## 5581. streetcar

- 뜻: 노면전차, 시가전차
- 예문: We rode the old streetcar through the busy downtown streets.

## 5582. holiness

- 뜻: 신성함, 거룩함
- 예문: The monks spoke of holiness as a way of daily living.

## 5583. growl

- 뜻: 으르렁거리다
- 예문: The dog began to growl when a stranger approached the gate.

## 5584. conform

- 뜻: 따르다, 순응하다
- 예문: Students must conform to the school's dress code during exams.

## 5585. conqueror

- 뜻: 정복자
- 예문: The conqueror demanded that the defeated city open its gates.

## 5586. daydream

- 뜻: 백일몽, 공상하다
- 예문: She would daydream about travel while sitting at her office desk.

## 5587. disable

- 뜻: 못 쓰게 하다, 장애를 입히다

## 5588. shield

- 뜻: 방패; 보호하다
- 예문: He raised his arm to shield his eyes from the bright sun.

## 5589. persecution

- 뜻: 박해
- 예문: Many families fled their country to escape political persecution at home.

## 5590. salesman

- 뜻: 판매원, 외판원
- 예문: The salesman showed us several cars before we chose one.

## 5591. battalion

- 뜻: (군대) 대대
- 예문: The battalion moved forward at dawn across the open field.

## 5592. beating

- 뜻: 구타, 두드림; 완패
- 예문: The boxer received a beating but still finished the entire match.

## 5593. benefactor

- 뜻: 후원자, 은인
- 예문: A generous benefactor donated money to rebuild the local library.

## 5594. alternate

- 뜻: 번갈아 하다, 교대의

## 5595. laureate

## 5596. jewel

## 5600. massage

- 예문: A short massage helped ease the tension in her tired shoulders.
