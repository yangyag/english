# 필수 영어단어 1201–1250

## 1201. interesting

- 뜻: 재미있는, 흥미로운
- 예문: The museum had an interesting exhibit about life in the city.

## 1202. football

- 뜻: 축구, 미식축구
- 예문: The children play football in the park after school every day.

## 1203. review

- 뜻: 검토하다, 복습하다; 리뷰, 검토
- 예문: Please review the report before the meeting tomorrow morning.

## 1204. wish

- 뜻: 바라다, 소원
- 예문: I wish I could visit my grandparents more often this year.

## 1205. resource

- 뜻: 자원, 자료
- 예문: Water is a precious resource that we should not waste.

## 1206. option

- 뜻: 선택, 선택권
- 예문: You have the option to pay now or later this month.

## 1207. topic

- 뜻: 주제, 화제
- 예문: The teacher asked each student to choose a topic for the essay.

## 1208. trend

- 뜻: 추세, 유행
- 예문: This new trend in fashion started on social media last year.

## 1209. prevent

- 뜻: 막다, 예방하다
- 예문: Washing your hands can prevent the spread of many diseases.

## 1210. spot

- 뜻: 장소, 얼룩; 알아보다
- 예문: We found a quiet spot by the river for our picnic.

## 1211. style

- 뜻: 스타일, 방식
- 예문: Her writing style is simple, clear, and easy to follow.

## 1212. connection

- 뜻: 연결, 관계
- 예문: There is a strong connection between sleep and good health.

## 1213. marriage

- 뜻: 결혼, 혼인
- 예문: They celebrated their marriage with family and friends last weekend.

## 1214. mental

- 뜻: 마음의, 정신의
- 예문: Regular exercise can also improve your mental health over time.

## 1215. access

- 뜻: 접근, 이용; 접근하다
- 예문: Students can access the library computers with their school cards.

## 1216. drug

- 뜻: 약, 마약
- 예문: The doctor prescribed a new drug to treat her infection.

## 1217. method

- 뜻: 방법, 방식
- 예문: This method of cooking rice is faster than the old one.

## 1218. belief

- 뜻: 믿음, 신념
- 예문: It is my belief that hard work leads to success.

## 1219. planet

- 뜻: 행성
- 예문: Earth is the only planet we know that supports human life.

## 1220. communication

- 뜻: 의사소통, 통신
- 예문: Good communication is important when you work with a team.

## 1221. replace

- 뜻: 교체하다, 대신하다
- 예문: We need to replace the broken window before winter arrives.

## 1222. lack

- 뜻: 부족, 결핍; 부족하다
- 예문: The project failed because of a lack of time and money.

## 1223. bill

- 뜻: 계산서, 청구서; 법안
- 예문: I paid the electricity bill at the bank this morning.

## 1224. president

- 뜻: 대통령, 회장
- 예문: The president of the company spoke to all new employees today.

## 1225. powerful

- 뜻: 강력한, 힘 있는
- 예문: The powerful storm knocked down several trees along the road.

## 1226. wild

- 뜻: 야생의, 거친
- 예문: We saw wild horses running across the open field.

## 1227. researcher

- 뜻: 연구원, 연구자
- 예문: The researcher spent years studying how plants grow in dry soil.

## 1228. forward

- 뜻: 앞으로; 전달하다
- 예문: Please move forward so that others can enter the room.

## 1229. single

- 뜻: 하나의, 독신의
- 예문: Not a single student was late for class this morning.

## 1230. partner

- 뜻: 파트너, 배우자, 동업자
- 예문: She and her business partner opened a small cafe last year.

## 1231. past

- 뜻: 과거; 지난
- 예문: In the past, people wrote letters instead of sending emails.

## 1232. advantage

- 뜻: 이점, 유리함
- 예문: Speaking two languages is a real advantage in many jobs.

## 1233. significant

- 뜻: 중요한, 상당한
- 예문: There has been a significant increase in the price of food.

## 1234. training

- 뜻: 훈련, 교육
- 예문: New staff must complete a week of training before they start.

## 1235. connect

- 뜻: 연결하다
- 예문: You can connect your phone to the speaker with a cable.

## 1236. reporter

- 뜻: 기자
- 예문: The reporter asked the mayor several questions after the meeting.

## 1237. policy

- 뜻: 정책, 방침
- 예문: The school has a strict policy against using phones in class.

## 1238. final

- 뜻: 최종의, 마지막의
- 예문: The final exam will cover everything we learned this semester.

## 1239. survive

- 뜻: 살아남다, 생존하다
- 예문: Many plants cannot survive without enough sunlight and water.

## 1240. weight

- 뜻: 무게, 체중
- 예문: Please check the weight of this box before you lift it.

## 1241. account

- 뜻: 계정, 계좌; 설명
- 예문: I opened a new bank account to save money for college.

## 1242. private

- 뜻: 사적인, 비공개의
- 예문: Please keep this information private and do not share it.

## 1243. reflect

- 뜻: 반영하다, 숙고하다; 반사하다
- 예문: Take a moment to reflect on what you learned today.

## 1244. associate

- 뜻: 관련짓다, 연합하다; 동료
- 예문: Many people associate the smell of rain with summer nights.

## 1245. reaction

- 뜻: 반응
- 예문: Her reaction to the surprise party was a huge smile.

## 1246. screen

- 뜻: 화면, 스크린
- 예문: He spent too many hours looking at a computer screen.

## 1247. text

- 뜻: 글, 문자; 문자를 보내다
- 예문: Please read the text on page ten before class starts.

## 1248. damage

- 뜻: 손상, 피해; 손상시키다
- 예문: The heavy rain caused a lot of damage to the roof.

## 1249. thus

- 뜻: 따라서, 이렇게
- 예문: The road was closed, thus we had to take another route.

## 1250. pain

- 뜻: 고통, 아픔
- 예문: She felt a sharp pain in her knee after the long walk.
