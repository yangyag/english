# 필수 영어단어 5101–5150

## 5101. supervision

- 뜻: 감독, 지도
- 예문: Children need adult supervision while they swim in the pool.

## 5102. deficiency

- 뜻: 결핍, 부족; 결함
- 예문: A vitamin deficiency can make you feel tired all day.

## 5103. disposal

- 뜻: 처리, 처분; 폐기
- 예문: Please follow the rules for safe disposal of old batteries.

## 5104. eighteenth

- 뜻: 열여덟 번째의
- 예문: She celebrated her eighteenth birthday with friends at home.

## 5105. faction

- 뜻: 파벌, 분파
- 예문: A small faction in the party opposed the new policy.

## 5106. apparatus

- 뜻: 장치, 기구
- 예문: The laboratory apparatus must stay clean after every experiment.

## 5107. directory

- 뜻: 주소록, 안내 명부; 디렉터리
- 예문: I found her office number in the company directory.

## 5108. synthesis

- 뜻: 합성, 종합
- 예문: The scientist described the chemical synthesis of a new compound.

## 5109. massacre

- 뜻: 학살, 대학살
- 예문: The museum exhibit remembers the victims of that massacre.

## 5110. applicant

- 뜻: 지원자, 신청자
- 예문: Each applicant must submit a resume and a cover letter.

## 5111. reside

- 뜻: 거주하다
- 예문: Many students reside in dormitories during their first year.

## 5112. conserve

- 뜻: 보존하다, 아끼다
- 예문: We should conserve water during the long summer drought.

## 5113. adjacent

- 뜻: 인접한, 이웃한
- 예문: Our house is adjacent to a quiet park and playground.

## 5114. gravely

- 뜻: 심각하게, 엄숙하게
- 예문: The doctor spoke gravely about the patient's sudden condition.

## 5115. magistrate

- 뜻: 치안판사
- 예문: The magistrate listened carefully before deciding the local case.

## 5116. notify

- 뜻: 통지하다, 알리다
- 예문: Please notify us immediately if your address has changed.

## 5117. devise

- 뜻: 고안하다, 궁리하다
- 예문: The team will devise a new plan to reduce costs.

## 5118. graphic

- 뜻: 생생한; 그래픽의, 도표의
- 예문: The report includes a graphic chart of monthly sales.

## 5119. formulate

- 뜻: 공식화하다, 명확히 표현하다
- 예문: Scientists formulate a hypothesis before they begin the experiment.

## 5120. optical

- 뜻: 광학의, 시각의
- 예문: She bought new optical lenses for her old camera.

## 5121. dual

- 뜻: 이중의, 두 개의
- 예문: The car has dual airbags to protect the front passengers.

## 5122. divert

- 뜻: 전환하다, 우회시키다
- 예문: Signs divert drivers onto a safer road around the flood.

## 5123. denounce

- 뜻: 비난하다, 규탄하다
- 예문: Leaders denounce violence and call for a peaceful solution.

## 5124. contention

- 뜻: 주장, 논쟁
- 예문: His main contention is that the test was unfair.

## 5125. generously

- 뜻: 관대하게, 후하게
- 예문: She generously offered her spare room to the visitors.

## 5126. anxiously

- 뜻: 걱정스럽게, 초조하게
- 예문: He waited anxiously for the exam results to arrive.

## 5127. applaud

- 뜻: 박수하다; 칭찬하다
- 예문: The audience stood to applaud the singers after the concert.

## 5128. firefighter

- 뜻: 소방관
- 예문: A firefighter rescued the cat from the burning apartment.

## 5129. inadequate

- 뜻: 불충분한, 부적절한
- 예문: The small tent was inadequate for such a cold night.

## 5130. firearm

- 뜻: 총기
- 예문: The museum displays an old firearm from the civil war.

## 5131. forge

- 뜻: 위조하다; 구축하다; 단조하다
- 예문: They hope to forge a lasting partnership with the new company.

## 5132. provincial

- 뜻: 지방의; 편협한
- 예문: She grew up in a quiet provincial town far from the capital.

## 5133. disastrous

- 뜻: 재앙적인, 비참한
- 예문: The disastrous storm left thousands of families without electricity.

## 5134. fixture

- 뜻: 고정 비품; 정기 행사
- 예문: We replaced the old light fixture in the dining room.

## 5135. editorial

- 뜻: 사설; 편집의
- 예문: The newspaper published an editorial about school lunch programs.

## 5136. realization

- 뜻: 깨달음; 실현
- 예문: The sudden realization that he was lost made him stop.

## 5137. marginal

- 뜻: 미미한, 주변의
- 예문: There was only a marginal increase in sales this quarter.

## 5138. notable

- 뜻: 주목할 만한, 저명한
- 예문: She is a notable scientist known for her climate research.

## 5139. inclusion

- 뜻: 포함; 포용
- 예문: The inclusion of fresh fruit made the lunch healthier.

## 5140. descent

- 뜻: 하강; 혈통
- 예문: The plane began its descent toward the airport at dusk.

## 5141. linear

- 뜻: 선형의, 일직선의
- 예문: The graph shows a linear increase in temperature over time.

## 5142. formally

- 뜻: 공식적으로, 형식적으로
- 예문: She was formally introduced to the guests at dinner.

## 5143. defect

- 뜻: 결함, 결점
- 예문: The company recalled the phones because of a software defect.

## 5144. junction

- 뜻: 교차점, 접합점
- 예문: Turn left at the junction next to the old post office.

## 5145. rebellion

- 뜻: 반란, 반항
- 예문: The farmers' rebellion against high taxes lasted several months.

## 5146. Prof

- 뜻: 교수 (약칭)
- 예문: Prof Kim will hold office hours after class on Friday.

## 5147. crave

- 뜻: 갈망하다, 간절히 원하다
- 예문: After a long hike I always crave a cold drink.

## 5148. rock'n'roll

- 뜻: 로큰롤
- 예문: They danced to classic rock'n'roll songs at the party.

## 5149. berry

- 뜻: 베리, 작은 열매
- 예문: She picked a ripe berry from the bush and tasted it.

## 5150. illiterate

- 뜻: 문맹의, 글을 모르는
- 예문: The program helps illiterate adults learn to read and write.
