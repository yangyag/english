# 필수 영어단어 951–1000

## 951. natural

- 뜻: 자연의, 자연스러운
- 예문: Her natural talent for music was obvious from the start.

## 952. share

- 뜻: 나누다, 공유하다; 몫
- 예문: Please share your notes with the rest of the class.

## 953. special

- 뜻: 특별한
- 예문: She cooked a special meal for her parents last night.

## 954. death

- 뜻: 죽음
- 예문: The family was shocked by the sudden death of their neighbor.

## 955. reach

- 뜻: 도달하다, 닿다
- 예문: They hope to reach the village before dark tonight.

## 956. amount

- 뜻: 양, 액수
- 예문: The amount of rain this month is higher than usual.

## 957. blood

- 뜻: 피, 혈액
- 예문: The nurse took a small sample of blood from his arm.

## 958. decide

- 뜻: 결정하다
- 예문: We need to decide where to go for dinner tonight.

## 959. case

- 뜻: 경우, 사건
- 예문: In this case, we should wait a little longer for them.

## 960. certainly

- 뜻: 확실히, 물론
- 예문: She will certainly call you as soon as she arrives.

## 961. attention

- 뜻: 주의, 관심
- 예문: Please pay attention to the safety rules during the tour.

## 962. continue

- 뜻: 계속하다
- 예문: We will continue the meeting after a short break.

## 963. sort

- 뜻: 종류; 분류하다
- 예문: What sort of music do you usually listen to?

## 964. cost

- 뜻: 비용; 비용이 들다
- 예문: How much does it cost to repair this old computer?

## 965. art

- 뜻: 예술, 미술
- 예문: She studies modern art at the university this semester.

## 966. bit

- 뜻: 조금, 작은 조각
- 예문: Could you wait a bit while I finish this email?

## 967. worker

- 뜻: 노동자, 직원
- 예문: Every worker in the factory received a small bonus.

## 968. force

- 뜻: 힘, 강제하다
- 예문: Do not force the door if the lock is stuck.

## 969. reveal

- 뜻: 드러내다, 밝히다
- 예문: The report will reveal the true cause of the accident.

## 970. population

- 뜻: 인구
- 예문: The population of this small town has grown quickly.

## 971. evidence

- 뜻: 증거
- 예문: The police found new evidence near the empty building.

## 972. value

- 뜻: 가치, 소중히 여기다
- 예문: This old watch has great value to our family.

## 973. disease

- 뜻: 질병, 병
- 예문: Doctors are working hard to stop the spread of the disease.

## 974. common

- 뜻: 흔한, 공통의
- 예문: Colds are common during the winter months at school.

## 975. figure

- 뜻: 숫자, 모습; 이해하다
- 예문: I cannot figure out the answer to this math problem.

## 976. role

- 뜻: 역할
- 예문: She plays an important role in the new project.

## 977. deal

- 뜻: 거래, 다루다
- 예문: We made a fair deal with the local car seller.

## 978. focus

- 뜻: 초점, 집중하다
- 예문: Please focus on your homework until dinner is ready.

## 979. order

- 뜻: 주문하다, 명령; 순서
- 예문: I would like to order a pizza for dinner tonight.

## 980. political

- 뜻: 정치의
- 예문: The students discussed several political issues after class yesterday.

## 981. mention

- 뜻: 언급하다
- 예문: Did she mention the meeting time in her last email?

## 982. couple

- 뜻: 둘, 한 쌍; 부부
- 예문: A young couple sat talking on the park bench.

## 983. recently

- 뜻: 최근에
- 예문: I recently started a new job at a small company.

## 984. personal

- 뜻: 개인의, 사적인
- 예문: Please do not open any personal letters on my desk.

## 985. either

- 뜻: 어느 한쪽; (부정문에서) ~도 역시
- 예문: You can take either bus to get to the station.

## 986. particular

- 뜻: 특정한, 특별한
- 예문: Is there a particular reason you want to leave early?

## 987. reduce

- 뜻: 줄이다, 감소시키다
- 예문: Try to reduce the amount of sugar in your tea.

## 988. science

- 뜻: 과학
- 예문: She wants to study science at college next year.

## 989. skill

- 뜻: 기술, 솜씨
- 예문: Cooking is a useful skill that anyone can learn.

## 990. local

- 뜻: 지역의, 현지의
- 예문: We buy fresh vegetables from the local market every Saturday.

## 991. rather

- 뜻: 오히려, 다소; ~하느니 차라리
- 예문: I would rather walk than wait for the next bus.

## 992. ability

- 뜻: 능력
- 예문: She has the ability to stay calm under pressure.

## 993. within

- 뜻: ~이내에, ~안에
- 예문: Please finish the report within two days if possible.

## 994. popular

- 뜻: 인기 있는
- 예문: This cafe is popular with students from the nearby school.

## 995. condition

- 뜻: 상태, 조건
- 예문: The used car is in good condition for its age.

## 996. colour

- 뜻: 색, 색깔
- 예문: What colour did you choose for the living room walls?

## 997. environment

- 뜻: 환경
- 예문: We should protect the environment for our children and grandchildren.

## 998. law

- 뜻: 법, 법률
- 예문: Everyone must follow the law, even in a small town.

## 999. industry

- 뜻: 산업
- 예문: Tourism is the main industry in this coastal town.

## 1000. ground

- 뜻: 땅, 지면
- 예문: The children sat on the ground under a large tree.
