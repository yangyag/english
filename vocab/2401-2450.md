# 필수 영어단어 2401–2450

## 2401. fixed

- 뜻: 고정된, 정해진, 수리된
- 예문: The meeting is at a fixed time every Monday morning.

## 2402. aircraft

- 뜻: 항공기, 비행기
- 예문: The small aircraft landed safely after the sudden storm.

## 2403. quantity

- 뜻: 양, 수량
- 예문: Please check the quantity of flour before you start baking.

## 2404. unknown

- 뜻: 알려지지 않은, 미지의
- 예문: The artist remained unknown until her work appeared online.

## 2405. protest

- 뜻: 항의하다, 시위하다; 항의, 시위
- 예문: Hundreds of people gathered downtown to protest the new law.

## 2406. insight

- 뜻: 통찰, 이해
- 예문: Her book offers real insight into how children learn language.

## 2407. unhappy

- 뜻: 불행한, 불만족한
- 예문: He felt unhappy about the sudden change in his schedule.

## 2408. consideration

- 뜻: 고려, 배려
- 예문: Please give this offer careful consideration before you decide.

## 2409. fifth

- 뜻: 다섯 번째의, 5분의 1
- 예문: She finished in fifth place after a very close race.

## 2410. install

- 뜻: 설치하다
- 예문: The technician will install the new software this afternoon.

## 2411. embarrassed

- 뜻: 당황한, 부끄러운
- 예문: I felt embarrassed when I called my teacher by the wrong name.

## 2412. routine

- 뜻: 일상, 루틴; 일상적인
- 예문: Exercise is part of her morning routine before work.

## 2413. headache

- 뜻: 두통, 골치 아픈 일
- 예문: Bright lights always give me a headache in the afternoon.

## 2414. satellite

- 뜻: 위성
- 예문: The weather satellite sent clear images of the approaching storm.

## 2415. advertising

- 뜻: 광고, 광고업
- 예문: The company spent a lot of money on advertising this year.

## 2416. rank

- 뜻: 계급, 순위; 순위를 매기다
- 예문: She quickly rose to a high rank in the army.

## 2417. fence

- 뜻: 울타리, 담
- 예문: They built a wooden fence around the backyard last weekend.

## 2418. bunch

- 뜻: 다발, 송이, 무리
- 예문: She bought a bunch of bananas at the corner market.

## 2419. dialogue

- 뜻: 대화, 대사
- 예문: The movie has a funny dialogue between the two main characters.

## 2420. steady

- 뜻: 안정된, 꾸준한
- 예문: Keep a steady pace if you want to finish the race.

## 2421. oven

- 뜻: 오븐, 화덕
- 예문: Put the cake in the oven for about thirty minutes.

## 2422. mineral

- 뜻: 광물, 미네랄
- 예문: Iron is an important mineral that the human body needs.

## 2423. deliberately

- 뜻: 고의로, 일부러
- 예문: He deliberately left the door open so the cat could enter.

## 2424. pale

- 뜻: 창백한, 옅은
- 예문: She looked pale after staying up all night to study.

## 2425. painting

- 뜻: 그림, 회화; 페인트칠
- 예문: A famous painting hangs above the fireplace in the hall.

## 2426. evening

- 뜻: 저녁, 저녁때
- 예문: We usually take a short walk in the evening after dinner.

## 2427. bowl

- 뜻: 그릇, 볼
- 예문: He poured soup into a large bowl and sat down.

## 2428. desert

- 뜻: 사막; 버리다
- 예문: They drove across the desert for hours without seeing a town.

## 2429. These

- 뜻: 이것들, 이들
- 예문: These shoes are more comfortable than the ones I bought yesterday.

## 2430. Christmas

- 뜻: 크리스마스
- 예문: Our family always gathers at my grandmother's house for Christmas.

## 2431. recognise

- 뜻: 알아보다, 인정하다
- 예문: I did not recognise him at first with that new haircut.

## 2432. Christian

- 뜻: 그리스도교의, 기독교인
- 예문: She grew up in a Christian family that went to church weekly.

## 2433. behavior

- 뜻: 행동, 행실
- 예문: The teacher praised the class for their calm behavior during the trip.

## 2434. anymore

- 뜻: 더 이상
- 예문: I do not watch that show anymore because it got boring.

## 2435. growing

- 뜻: 증가하는, 자라는
- 예문: There is a growing demand for cheaper housing in the city.

## 2436. carbon

- 뜻: 탄소
- 예문: Trees take carbon from the air and help slow climate change.

## 2437. DNA

- 뜻: 디엔에이, 유전자 정보
- 예문: Scientists compared the DNA from the two plants in the lab.

## 2438. Spanish

- 뜻: 스페인의, 스페인어
- 예문: She is taking Spanish classes twice a week after work.

## 2439. perceive

- 뜻: 인식하다, 지각하다
- 예문: Many people perceive the new policy as unfair to small shops.

## 2440. emphasise

- 뜻: 강조하다
- 예문: The coach tried to emphasise the importance of rest after games.

## 2441. cope

- 뜻: 대처하다, 견디다
- 예문: She learned to cope with stress by taking short walks.

## 2442. naked

- 뜻: 벌거벗은, 적나라한
- 예문: The baby ran naked through the house after his bath.

## 2443. cognitive

- 뜻: 인지의, 사고의
- 예문: Reading every day can support cognitive development in young children.

## 2444. sigh

- 뜻: 한숨; 한숨 쉬다
- 예문: She let out a long sigh after finishing the difficult exam.

## 2445. discrimination

- 뜻: 차별, 구별
- 예문: The company has strict rules against discrimination in the workplace.

## 2446. crack

- 뜻: 금, 균열; 금이 가다
- 예문: There is a small crack in the window after last night's storm.

## 2447. proportion

- 뜻: 비율, 부분
- 예문: A large proportion of the students walk to school each day.

## 2448. nod

- 뜻: 고개를 끄덕이다; 끄덕임
- 예문: He gave a quick nod when I asked if he understood.

## 2449. divorce

- 뜻: 이혼; 이혼하다
- 예문: They decided to get a divorce after many years of arguments.

## 2450. controversial

- 뜻: 논란이 되는, 논쟁적인
- 예문: The mayor made a controversial decision about closing the old park.
