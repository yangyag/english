# 필수 영어단어 1501–1550

## 1501. suit

- 뜻: 정장; 어울리다; 소송
- 예문: He wore a dark suit to the job interview yesterday.

## 1502. zone

- 뜻: 지대, 구역
- 예문: This quiet street is a school zone, so drive slowly.

## 1503. realize

- 뜻: 깨닫다, 알아차리다; 실현하다
- 예문: I did not realize how late it was until now.

## 1504. found

- 뜻: 설립하다, 창립하다
- 예문: They decided to found a club for young artists in town.

## 1505. court

- 뜻: 법원, 법정; 코트
- 예문: The lawyer asked the court to delay the trial.

## 1506. observe

- 뜻: 관찰하다, 지켜보다
- 예문: Scientists observe the stars through a large telescope at night.

## 1507. match

- 뜻: 경기; 어울리다
- 예문: Our team won the match after a very close game.

## 1508. guest

- 뜻: 손님, 게스트
- 예문: We prepared a spare room for our guest from Busan.

## 1509. define

- 뜻: 정의하다, 규정하다
- 예문: Can you define this word in a simple way?

## 1510. currently

- 뜻: 현재, 지금
- 예문: She is currently working on a new project at home.

## 1511. seriously

- 뜻: 진지하게, 심각하게
- 예문: You should take this warning seriously and drive more carefully.

## 1512. nobody

- 뜻: 아무도 ~않다
- 예문: Nobody knew the answer to the last question.

## 1513. thanks

- 뜻: 고마워, 감사
- 예문: Thanks for helping me carry these heavy boxes upstairs.

## 1514. literature

- 뜻: 문학, 문헌
- 예문: She studies English literature at a university in Seoul.

## 1515. respect

- 뜻: 존경, 존중; 존경하다
- 예문: Students should respect their teachers and classmates at school.

## 1516. officer

- 뜻: 경찰관, 장교, 공무원
- 예문: A police officer stopped the car at the busy corner.

## 1517. traffic

- 뜻: 교통, 교통량
- 예문: Heavy traffic made us late for the morning meeting.

## 1518. northern

- 뜻: 북쪽의, 북부의
- 예문: They spent the summer in a small northern town near the lake.

## 1519. cycle

- 뜻: 순환, 주기; 자전거를 타다
- 예문: The seasons follow a regular cycle every year.

## 1520. code

- 뜻: 암호, 코드; 규범
- 예문: Please enter the code to unlock the front door.

## 1521. aim

- 뜻: 목표; 겨누다, 노리다
- 예문: Our aim is to finish the report before Friday.

## 1522. recommend

- 뜻: 추천하다, 권하다
- 예문: I recommend this restaurant if you like spicy food.

## 1523. highly

- 뜻: 매우, 높이
- 예문: This book is highly popular among young readers this year.

## 1524. reader

- 뜻: 독자, 읽는 사람
- 예문: The author thanked every reader at the end of the book.

## 1525. appearance

- 뜻: 외모, 모습; 출현
- 예문: His tired appearance showed that he had slept very little.

## 1526. weapon

- 뜻: 무기
- 예문: The museum displayed an old weapon from the last war.

## 1527. rely

- 뜻: 의지하다, 의존하다
- 예문: You can rely on her to finish the work on time.

## 1528. investigate

- 뜻: 조사하다, 수사하다
- 예문: The police will investigate the accident near the station.

## 1529. breathe

- 뜻: 숨 쉬다, 호흡하다
- 예문: Sit down and breathe slowly until you feel calm again.

## 1530. driver

- 뜻: 운전자, 기사
- 예문: The bus driver waited until every passenger sat down.

## 1531. teenager

- 뜻: 십 대, 청소년
- 예문: The teenager saved money to buy a used bicycle.

## 1532. increasingly

- 뜻: 점점 더, 더욱더
- 예문: People are increasingly working from home these days.

## 1533. sight

- 뜻: 광경, 시력
- 예문: The mountain view was a beautiful sight after the rain.

## 1534. bank

- 뜻: 은행; 강둑
- 예문: She opened a savings account at the bank last Monday.

## 1535. doubt

- 뜻: 의심; 의심하다
- 예문: I have no doubt that she will pass the exam.

## 1536. creature

- 뜻: 생물, 동물
- 예문: A strange creature appeared in the story she read.

## 1537. fund

- 뜻: 자금, 기금; 자금을 대다
- 예문: The school started a fund to help students buy books.

## 1538. characteristic

- 뜻: 특성, 특징; 특유의
- 예문: Patience is an important characteristic of a good teacher.

## 1539. bike

- 뜻: 자전거, 오토바이
- 예문: He rides his bike to work when the weather is nice.

## 1540. performance

- 뜻: 공연, 실적, 수행
- 예문: The school play was a wonderful performance last night.

## 1541. height

- 뜻: 높이, 키
- 예문: The height of that building is over one hundred meters.

## 1542. combination

- 뜻: 결합, 조합
- 예문: This dish is a combination of rice, eggs, and vegetables.

## 1543. alive

- 뜻: 살아 있는
- 예문: The lost dog was found alive behind the grocery store.

## 1544. core

- 뜻: 핵심, 중심
- 예문: Honesty is at the core of a strong friendship.

## 1545. necessary

- 뜻: 필요한, 필수적인
- 예문: A passport is necessary if you want to travel abroad.

## 1546. legal

- 뜻: 법률의, 합법적인
- 예문: You should get legal advice before signing that contract.

## 1547. indicate

- 뜻: 나타내다, 가리키다
- 예문: The signs indicate that the museum is closed on Mondays.

## 1548. honest

- 뜻: 정직한, 솔직한
- 예문: Please be honest with me about what really happened.

## 1549. citizen

- 뜻: 시민, 국민
- 예문: Every citizen has the right to vote in this country.

## 1550. shock

- 뜻: 충격; 충격을 주다
- 예문: The sudden news came as a shock to the whole family.
