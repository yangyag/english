# 필수 영어단어 5901–5950

## 5901. physic

- 뜻: 약, 의학; 하제
- 예문: The old doctor prescribed a physic for her persistent cough.

## 5902. docile

- 뜻: 온순한, 다루기 쉬운
- 예문: The docile horse stood still while the children brushed its coat.

## 5903. fidelity

- 뜻: 충실, 정절; 충실도
- 예문: Loyalty and fidelity kept their friendship strong through difficult years.

## 5904. manor

- 뜻: 장원, 영지의 저택
- 예문: The old manor stood among tall trees at the valley's edge.

## 5905. punctual

- 뜻: 시간을 잘 지키는, 정확한
- 예문: Please be punctual because the train leaves at exactly eight.

## 5906. circus

- 뜻: 서커스
- 예문: We took the children to the circus for their birthday treat.

## 5907. contradict

- 뜻: 반박하다, 모순되다
- 예문: His later statement seemed to contradict everything he had said earlier.

## 5908. audible

- 뜻: 들리는, 가청의
- 예문: Her whisper was barely audible in the noisy crowded hallway.

## 5909. auditor

- 뜻: 감사인; 청강생
- 예문: An independent auditor reviewed the company's books before the annual meeting.

## 5910. procure

- 뜻: 조달하다, 입수하다
- 예문: The chef had to procure fresh herbs from the market nearby.

## 5911. apprehend

- 뜻: 체포하다; 이해하다
- 예문: Officers hope to apprehend the thief before he crosses the border.

## 5912. comprehend

- 뜻: 이해하다, 파악하다
- 예문: Young children often cannot comprehend why the rules must change.

## 5913. fracture

- 뜻: 골절, 부러짐
- 예문: He suffered a fracture in his wrist after slipping on ice.

## 5914. cascade

- 뜻: 폭포; 연속해서 쏟아지다
- 예문: A cascade of water rushed down the rocks after heavy rain.

## 5915. chaste

- 뜻: 정숙한, 순결한
- 예문: The novel describes a chaste love that never turns into passion.

## 5916. invincible

- 뜻: 무적의, 지지 않는
- 예문: Many fans believed their team was invincible after the winning streak.

## 5917. omnibus

- 뜻: 종합의; 작품 모음집
- 예문: I bought an omnibus of her complete short stories last week.

## 5918. ode

- 뜻: 송가, 송시
- 예문: The poet recited an ode to the river at the festival.

## 5919. auxiliary

- 뜻: 보조의, 부가의
- 예문: An auxiliary engine kept the ship moving after the main failure.

## 5920. aviation

- 뜻: 항공, 비행
- 예문: She studied aviation because she had always dreamed of flying.

## 5921. aviator

- 뜻: 비행사, 조종사
- 예문: The aviator checked the maps before taking off at sunrise.

## 5922. inaugurate

- 뜻: 취임시키다; 개시하다
- 예문: They will inaugurate the new library with a public ceremony tomorrow.

## 5923. profane

- 뜻: 불경한, 세속의; 모독하다
- 예문: Please do not use profane language around the younger children.

## 5924. geology

- 뜻: 지질학
- 예문: She majored in geology and now maps rock layers in the field.

## 5925. satire

- 뜻: 풍자, 풍자 문학
- 예문: His satire of office life made the whole staff laugh aloud.

## 5926. assent

- 뜻: 동의, 찬성
- 예문: The council gave its assent to the plan after a short debate.

## 5927. brood

- 뜻: 한배의 새끼; 곰곰이 생각하다
- 예문: Do not brood over one mistake for the rest of the week.

## 5928. epitaph

- 뜻: 묘비명
- 예문: Visitors paused to read the epitaph carved into the old stone.

## 5929. abridge

- 뜻: 축약하다, 단축하다
- 예문: The publisher asked her to abridge the novel for young readers.

## 5930. lowly

- 뜻: 낮은, 비천한, 겸손한
- 예문: He began in a lowly position and slowly earned greater trust.

## 5931. discern

- 뜻: 식별하다, 알아보다
- 예문: I could barely discern the path through the thick evening mist.

## 5932. abnormal

- 뜻: 비정상적인, 이상한
- 예문: The lab flagged several abnormal results and asked for another test.

## 5933. abbot

- 뜻: 수도원장
- 예문: The abbot led evening prayers in the quiet stone chapel.

## 5934. par

- 뜻: 동등, 기준; (골프) 파
- 예문: Her golf score finished two under par on a windy afternoon.

## 5935. perennial

- 뜻: 다년생의; 지속적인
- 예문: Flooding is a perennial problem along this stretch of river.

## 5936. perplex

- 뜻: 당황하게 하다, 혼란스럽게 하다
- 예문: The mixed instructions only perplex new workers on their first day.

## 5937. nameless

- 뜻: 이름 없는, 익명의
- 예문: A nameless donor paid for the new books in the library.

## 5938. dome

- 뜻: 돔, 둥근 지붕
- 예문: Light poured through the glass dome high above the museum hall.

## 5939. submarine

- 뜻: 잠수함; 수중의
- 예문: The submarine remained hidden beneath the waves for several hours.

## 5940. belle

- 뜻: 미인, 미녀
- 예문: She was the belle of the party in her red silk dress.

## 5941. concord

- 뜻: 화합, 일치
- 예문: The neighbors lived in concord after settling the long fence dispute.

## 5942. cordial

- 뜻: 친절한, 따뜻한
- 예문: The host offered a cordial greeting as we entered the house.

## 5943. animate

- 뜻: 생기를 주다; 살아 있는
- 예문: A good teacher can animate even the driest history lesson.

## 5944. unanimous

- 뜻: 만장일치의
- 예문: The board reached a unanimous vote to hire the new director.

## 5945. weakest

- 뜻: 가장 약한
- 예문: The weakest player on the team still scored the winning goal.

## 5946. synchronize

- 뜻: 동기화하다, 동시에 맞추다
- 예문: We must synchronize our clocks before the timed experiment begins.

## 5947. cipher

- 뜻: 암호; 숫자 0
- 예문: She wrote the message in cipher so strangers could not read it.

## 5948. ply

- 뜻: 왕복하다; (일에) 종사하다; 겹
- 예문: Small boats ply the river between the market and the island.

## 5949. focal

- 뜻: 초점의, 중심이 되는
- 예문: Climate policy is a focal issue in this year's campaign.

## 5950. tract

- 뜻: 넓은 지역; 소책자
- 예문: Developers bought a large tract of farmland east of town.
