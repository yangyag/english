# 필수 영어단어 2101–2150

## 2101. prison

- 뜻: 감옥, 교도소
- 예문: The thief spent two years in prison for the robbery.

## 2102. potentially

- 뜻: 잠재적으로, 혹시 그럴 수도 있게
- 예문: This new medicine could potentially save thousands of lives.

## 2103. tone

- 뜻: 어조, 음색, 분위기
- 예문: Please keep a polite tone when you speak to customers.

## 2104. retirement

- 뜻: 은퇴, 퇴직
- 예문: After retirement, he plans to travel with his wife.

## 2105. significantly

- 뜻: 상당히, 현저히
- 예문: Prices have risen significantly since the beginning of the year.

## 2106. persuade

- 뜻: 설득하다
- 예문: She tried to persuade her parents to let her study abroad.

## 2107. relief

- 뜻: 안도, 안심; 구제, 경감
- 예문: We felt great relief when the missing child was found.

## 2108. facility

- 뜻: 시설, 설비
- 예문: The new sports facility will open to the public next month.

## 2109. offensive

- 뜻: 모욕적인, 공격적인; 공격
- 예문: Some viewers found the comedian's jokes offensive and walked out.

## 2110. successfully

- 뜻: 성공적으로
- 예문: The team successfully completed the project ahead of schedule.

## 2111. soul

- 뜻: 영혼, 마음
- 예문: Music can touch the soul in a powerful way.

## 2112. settle

- 뜻: 정착하다; 해결하다, 합의하다
- 예문: They hope to settle the dispute before the trial begins.

## 2113. conference

- 뜻: 회의, 학회
- 예문: She will present her research at an international conference next week.

## 2114. mouse

- 뜻: 쥐; (컴퓨터) 마우스
- 예문: A small mouse ran across the kitchen floor last night.

## 2115. addition

- 뜻: 추가, 덧셈
- 예문: In addition to rice, we served soup and fresh salad.

## 2116. ethical

- 뜻: 윤리적인, 도덕적인
- 예문: Doctors must follow strict ethical rules when treating patients.

## 2117. profile

- 뜻: 프로필, 인물 소개; 윤곽
- 예문: Please update your profile with a recent photo and bio.

## 2118. debt

- 뜻: 빚, 부채
- 예문: He worked two jobs to pay off his student debt.

## 2119. so-called

- 뜻: 소위, 이른바
- 예문: The so-called expert had never actually studied the subject.

## 2120. gift

- 뜻: 선물; 재능
- 예문: She wrapped a small gift for her friend's birthday.

## 2121. regulation

- 뜻: 규제, 규정
- 예문: The new safety regulation requires workers to wear helmets.

## 2122. disability

- 뜻: 장애, 장애 상태
- 예문: The building has ramps for people with a physical disability.

## 2123. calculate

- 뜻: 계산하다, 산출하다
- 예문: Please calculate the total cost before we order the supplies.

## 2124. creation

- 뜻: 창조, 창작물
- 예문: The artist's latest creation hung on the gallery wall.

## 2125. scream

- 뜻: 비명을 지르다; 비명
- 예문: The children scream with joy when the fireworks begin.

## 2126. lovely

- 뜻: 아름다운, 사랑스러운

## 2127. improvement

- 뜻: 개선, 향상
- 예문: There has been a clear improvement in her writing this year.

## 2128. crew

- 뜻: 승무원, 작업반, 팀
- 예문: The film crew arrived early to set up the cameras.

## 2129. oppose

- 뜻: 반대하다, 맞서다
- 예문: Many residents oppose the plan to build a new highway.

## 2130. yield

- 뜻: 산출하다, 양보하다; 수확량
- 예문: This investment should yield a modest profit within two years.

## 2131. awareness

- 뜻: 인식, 자각
- 예문: The campaign raised public awareness of the dangers of smoking.

## 2132. inquiry

- 뜻: 문의, 조사
- 예문: She sent an inquiry about the job opening last week.

## 2133. asleep

- 뜻: 잠든
- 예문: The baby finally fell asleep after a long car ride.

## 2134. scheme

- 뜻: 계획, 제도; 음모
- 예문: The city launched a new scheme to reduce traffic downtown.

## 2135. defend

- 뜻: 방어하다, 변호하다
- 예문: The lawyer will defend her client in court tomorrow morning.

## 2136. widely

- 뜻: 널리, 폭넓게
- 예문: The book is widely read by students around the world.

## 2137. enormous

- 뜻: 거대한, 막대한
- 예문: They spent an enormous amount of money on the wedding.

## 2138. previously

- 뜻: 이전에, 예전에
- 예문: She had previously worked as a nurse in a small clinic.

## 2139. brilliant

- 뜻: 훌륭한, 뛰어난; 빛나는
- 예문: The young pianist gave a brilliant performance at the concert.

## 2140. reliable

- 뜻: 믿을 수 있는, 신뢰할 만한

## 2141. stare

- 뜻: 응시하다, 빤히 보다

## 2142. roughly

- 뜻: 대략, 대충; 거칠게

## 2143. bacteria

- 뜻: 박테리아, 세균

## 2144. achievement

- 뜻: 성취, 업적

## 2147. evil
