# 필수 영어단어 601–650

## 601. cloud

- 뜻: 구름; 클라우드
- 예문: A dark cloud covered the sun before the rain started.

## 602. tomato

- 뜻: 토마토
- 예문: She sliced a ripe tomato for the salad at lunch.

## 603. breakfast

- 뜻: 아침식사
- 예문: We usually eat breakfast together before leaving for work.

## 604. stupid

- 뜻: 어리석은, 바보 같은
- 예문: It was stupid to go outside without a coat.

## 605. square

- 뜻: 정사각형; 광장
- 예문: They met in the town square after the movie.

## 606. grandmother

- 뜻: 할머니

## 608. dirty

## 609. say

- 뜻: 말하다
- 예문: What did she say about the meeting tomorrow morning?

## 610. well

- 뜻: 잘; 우물; 글쎄

## 611. just

- 뜻: 단지, 방금; 공정한

## 612. minute

- 뜻: 분, 잠깐

## 613. sound

## 614. top

## 616. arm

## 617. rock

## 618. shoot

- 뜻: 쏘다; 촬영하다

## 619. blow

- 뜻: 불다; 강타

## 620. son

- 뜻: 아들

## 621. church

## 622. ball

## 623. corner

## 630. knee

## 631. shut

- 예문: Please shut the door because it is getting cold.

## 632. mirror

## 633. nurse

## 635. tape
