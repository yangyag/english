# 필수 영어단어 1051–1100

## 1051. raise

- 뜻: 올리다, 기르다
- 예문: Please raise your hand if you have a question.

## 1052. character

- 뜻: 성격, 인물; 문자
- 예문: The main character in this story is a brave girl.

## 1053. discover

- 뜻: 발견하다, 알아내다
- 예문: Scientists hope to discover a new treatment for this disease.

## 1054. protect

- 뜻: 보호하다, 지키다
- 예문: Wear a helmet to protect your head while you ride.

## 1055. positive

- 뜻: 긍정적인, 확실한
- 예문: She stayed positive even after the team lost the game.

## 1056. teacher

- 뜻: 교사, 선생님
- 예문: My teacher explained the math problem again after class.

## 1057. treat

- 뜻: 대하다, 치료하다
- 예문: Please treat everyone in this class with respect and kindness.

## 1058. leader

- 뜻: 지도자, 리더
- 예문: The team chose a new leader after the coach retired.

## 1059. huge

- 뜻: 거대한, 엄청난
- 예문: A huge crowd waited outside the stadium before the concert.

## 1060. stuff

- 뜻: 물건, 것
- 예문: Please put your stuff in the locker during gym class.

## 1061. ten

- 뜻: 열, 열 개의
- 예문: We need ten chairs for the meeting this afternoon.

## 1062. machine

- 뜻: 기계
- 예문: This machine can print hundreds of pages in one hour.

## 1063. success

- 뜻: 성공
- 예문: Hard work was the key to her success in school.

## 1064. traditional

- 뜻: 전통적인
- 예문: They served traditional food at the festival last weekend.

## 1065. building

- 뜻: 건물, 건축
- 예문: The new office building has ten floors and a cafe.

## 1066. fear

- 뜻: 두려움; 두려워하다
- 예문: She tried to hide her fear before the big test.

## 1067. inside

- 뜻: 안, 내부에
- 예문: Please wait inside the lobby until your name is called.

## 1068. education

- 뜻: 교육
- 예문: Good education can open many doors later in life.

## 1069. opportunity

- 뜻: 기회
- 예문: This internship is a great opportunity to learn new skills.

## 1070. source

- 뜻: 출처, 원천
- 예문: Always check the source of the news before you share it.

## 1071. conversation

- 뜻: 대화, 회화
- 예문: We had a long conversation about our plans after dinner.

## 1072. compare

- 뜻: 비교하다
- 예문: Compare the two phones before you decide which one to buy.

## 1073. position

- 뜻: 위치, 직위, 입장
- 예문: She applied for a new position at the company last week.

## 1074. current

- 뜻: 현재의; 전류
- 예문: The current price of the ticket is higher than last year.

## 1075. generation

- 뜻: 세대
- 예문: Young people in this generation spend more time online.

## 1076. imagine

- 뜻: 상상하다
- 예문: Can you imagine living in a small village by the sea?

## 1077. image

- 뜻: 이미지, 영상, 인상
- 예문: The image on the screen was too dark to see clearly.

## 1078. knowledge

- 뜻: 지식
- 예문: She has a deep knowledge of history and world cultures.

## 1079. rate

- 뜻: 비율, 요금; 평가하다
- 예문: The interest rate on this loan is lower than expected.

## 1080. save

- 뜻: 구하다, 저축하다, 저장하다
- 예문: I try to save a little money from every paycheck.

## 1081. general

- 뜻: 일반적인; 장군
- 예문: The general idea of the plan is easy to understand.

## 1082. college

- 뜻: 대학, 단과대학
- 예문: She wants to go to college after she finishes high school.

## 1083. refer

- 뜻: 언급하다, 참조하다
- 예문: Please refer to the map if you get lost downtown.

## 1084. official

- 뜻: 공식적인; 공무원
- 예문: The official results of the contest will come out tomorrow.

## 1085. myself

- 뜻: 나 자신
- 예문: I made this cake myself for my sister's birthday.

## 1086. challenge

- 뜻: 도전, 과제; 도전하다
- 예문: Learning a new language is a challenge, but it is fun.

## 1087. available

- 뜻: 이용할 수 있는, 가능한
- 예문: The tickets will be available online after nine o'clock.

## 1088. main

- 뜻: 주요한, 주된
- 예문: The main reason for the delay was heavy rain.

## 1089. clear

- 뜻: 맑은, 분명한; 치우다
- 예문: The sky is clear, so we can see the mountains today.

## 1090. individual

- 뜻: 개인의, 개인
- 예문: Each individual student must complete the form before Friday.

## 1091. economic

- 뜻: 경제의, 경제적인
- 예문: The economic news this week has worried many small businesses.

## 1092. message

- 뜻: 메시지, 전언
- 예문: I left a message for you on your office desk.

## 1093. outside

- 뜻: 밖, 바깥
- 예문: The children played outside until it started to rain.

## 1094. cell

- 뜻: 세포; 감방
- 예문: Every plant cell has a wall that gives it shape.

## 1095. impact

- 뜻: 영향, 충격
- 예문: The new law will have a big impact on small shops.

## 1096. theory

- 뜻: 이론

## 1097. concern

- 뜻: 걱정, 관심사; 관련되다

## 1098. somebody

- 뜻: 누군가

## 1099. skin

- 뜻: 피부, 껍질

## 1100. completely

- 뜻: 완전히, 전적으로
