# 필수 영어단어 3501–3550

## 3501. scandal

- 뜻: 스캔들, 추문
- 예문: The mayor resigned after the scandal appeared in the morning papers.

## 3502. midst

- 뜻: 한가운데, 중간
- 예문: She remained calm in the midst of the crowded train station.

## 3503. cave

- 뜻: 동굴
- 예문: The hikers found a dark cave near the mountain trail.

## 3504. curious

- 뜻: 호기심 많은; 이상한
- 예문: The curious child asked many questions about the night sky.

## 3505. spice

- 뜻: 향신료
- 예문: She added a little spice to the soup for extra flavor.

## 3506. salty

- 뜻: 짠, 소금기의
- 예문: The soup tasted too salty, so we added more water.

## 3507. deaf

- 뜻: 귀가 먼, 청각 장애의
- 예문: He has been deaf in one ear since childhood.

## 3508. forecast

- 뜻: 예보, 예측하다
- 예문: The weather forecast says rain will continue through the weekend.

## 3509. tide

- 뜻: 조수, 밀물과 썰물; 흐름
- 예문: The tide came in and covered the footprints on the beach.

## 3510. leisure

- 뜻: 여가, 한가한 시간

## 3511. shortage

- 뜻: 부족, 결핍
- 예문: There is a shortage of nurses at the local hospital.

## 3512. shrink

- 뜻: 줄어들다, 수축하다

## 3513. pat

- 뜻: 가볍게 두드리다, 쓰다듬다

## 3514. journalism

- 뜻: 저널리즘, 언론

## 3515. exceed

- 뜻: 넘다, 초과하다

## 3516. dense

- 뜻: 빽빽한, 밀도가 높은

## 3517. likelihood

- 뜻: 가능성
- 예문: There is little likelihood of rain during the outdoor concert.

## 3518. confusion

- 뜻: 혼란, 혼동
- 예문: The sudden schedule change caused confusion among the waiting passengers.

## 3519. cutting

- 뜻: 자르기, 절단; 신랄한
- 예문: He used a sharp knife for cutting the vegetables.

## 3520. advert

- 뜻: 광고
- 예문: I saw an advert for cheap flights on the bus.

## 3521. packet

- 뜻: 작은 봉지, 패킷
- 예문: She opened a packet of biscuits for the afternoon tea.

## 3522. artwork

- 뜻: 예술 작품, 삽화
- 예문: The museum displayed new artwork by local painters this month.

## 3523. enjoyment

- 뜻: 즐거움, 향유
- 예문: He takes great enjoyment in cooking dinner for his family.

## 3524. mature

- 뜻: 성숙한, 어른스러운
- 예문: She is mature for her age and handles problems calmly.

## 3525. secretly

- 뜻: 비밀리에, 몰래
- 예문: They secretly planned a surprise party for their teacher.

## 3526. goodness

- 뜻: 선량함, 착함
- 예문: Her goodness showed in the way she treated every stranger.

## 3527. offspring

- 뜻: 자식, 새끼
- 예문: The bird brought food to its offspring in the nest.

## 3528. noticeable

- 뜻: 눈에 띄는, 뚜렷한
- 예문: There was a noticeable change in his attitude after the meeting.

## 3529. conquer

- 뜻: 정복하다, 극복하다
- 예문: She finally managed to conquer her fear of public speaking.

## 3530. brake

- 뜻: 브레이크, 제동기
- 예문: She pressed the brake as the traffic light turned red.

## 3531. patron

- 뜻: 후원자, 단골
- 예문: He is a regular patron of the coffee shop downtown.

## 3532. coming

- 뜻: 다가오는, 앞으로의
- 예문: We are looking forward to the coming holiday weekend.

## 3533. illusion

- 뜻: 착각, 환상
- 예문: The magician created the illusion of a floating table.

## 3534. forbid

- 뜻: 금지하다, 금하다
- 예문: School rules forbid students from using phones during class.

## 3535. exhaust

- 뜻: 지치게 하다; 다 쓰다; 배기가스

## 3536. sofa

- 뜻: 소파
- 예문: The cat stretched out on the sofa and fell asleep.

## 3537. sixth

- 뜻: 여섯 번째의
- 예문: She finished in sixth place in the school race.

- 뜻: 총액의; 역겨운

## 3541. dock

- 뜻: 부두; 정박하다
- 예문: The ship will dock at the harbor later this afternoon.

## 3542. palm

- 뜻: 손바닥; 야자수

## 3544. satisfying

- 뜻: 만족스러운

## 3545. comfortably
