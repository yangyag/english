# 필수 영어단어 2451–2500

## 2451. bat

- 뜻: 박쥐; 방망이
- 예문: He swung the bat and hit the ball over the fence.

## 2452. revenue

- 뜻: 수익, 수입
- 예문: The company reported higher revenue this quarter than last year.

## 2453. norm

- 뜻: 규범, 표준, 일반적인 것
- 예문: Wearing a helmet is the norm among cyclists in this city.

## 2454. trace

- 뜻: 흔적; 추적하다
- 예문: Police could not find a trace of the missing package.

## 2455. morally

- 뜻: 도덕적으로
- 예문: She felt morally obligated to tell the truth about the accident.

## 2456. leadership

- 뜻: 리더십, 지도력
- 예문: Strong leadership helped the team finish the project on time.

## 2457. means

- 뜻: 수단, 방법; ~을 의미하다
- 예문: A bicycle is her only means of getting to work.

## 2458. refugee

- 뜻: 난민
- 예문: The refugee family found a safe home in a new country.

## 2459. favor

- 뜻: 호의, 부탁; 편들다
- 예문: Could you do me a favor and water my plants?

## 2460. absorb

- 뜻: 흡수하다; 몰두하다
- 예문: A thick towel can absorb a lot of water after a spill.

## 2461. physically

- 뜻: 신체적으로, 물리적으로
- 예문: The hike was physically demanding, but everyone reached the summit.

## 2462. trigger

- 뜻: 유발하다; 방아쇠
- 예문: Loud noises can trigger headaches for people who have migraines.

## 2463. tournament

- 뜻: 토너먼트, 대회
- 예문: Our school team won the basketball tournament last weekend.

## 2464. goodbye

- 뜻: 안녕, 작별 인사
- 예문: She waved goodbye to her friends at the train station.

## 2465. mainstream

- 뜻: 주류의, 대세의; 주류
- 예문: The once unusual idea has now become part of mainstream culture.

## 2466. workplace

- 뜻: 직장, 작업장
- 예문: Clear rules help everyone feel safer in the workplace.

## 2467. scare

- 뜻: 겁주다, 놀라게 하다; 공포
- 예문: The sudden thunder did not scare the children this time.

## 2468. website

- 뜻: 웹사이트
- 예문: Please visit our website for more information about the event.

## 2469. traditionally

- 뜻: 전통적으로
- 예문: Families traditionally gather for a big meal on New Year's Day.

## 2470. app

- 뜻: 앱, 애플리케이션
- 예문: I downloaded a new app to track my daily steps.

## 2471. hopefully

- 뜻: 바라건대, 희망을 가지고
- 예문: Hopefully the rain will stop before the picnic begins.

## 2472. script

- 뜻: 대본, 스크립트
- 예문: The actors practiced the script late into the night.

## 2473. Ms

- 뜻: ~씨 (혼인 여부와 상관없이 쓰는 여성 호칭)
- 예문: Please send the letter to Ms Park at the main office.

## 2474. ancestor

- 뜻: 조상
- 예문: Her ancestor came to this country more than a century ago.

## 2475. chin

- 뜻: 턱
- 예문: He rested his chin on his hand and thought for a moment.

## 2476. motivate

- 뜻: 동기를 부여하다
- 예문: Good teachers know how to motivate students to keep learning.

## 2477. ghost

- 뜻: 유령, 귀신
- 예문: The kids told ghost stories around the campfire after dark.

## 2478. immune

- 뜻: 면역의; 영향을 받지 않는
- 예문: After the vaccine, she was immune to that strain of the virus.

## 2479. regulate

- 뜻: 규제하다, 조절하다
- 예문: The government will regulate how companies use customer data.

## 2480. stroke

- 뜻: 뇌졸중; 쓰다듬다; (수영) 영법
- 예문: He recovered well after a stroke last winter with daily therapy.

## 2481. optimistic

- 뜻: 낙관적인
- 예문: She remains optimistic about finding a better job this year.

## 2482. unite

- 뜻: 연합하다, 단결하다
- 예문: Neighbors will unite to clean the park this Saturday morning.

## 2483. protein

- 뜻: 단백질
- 예문: Eggs and beans are cheap sources of protein for everyday meals.

## 2484. invisible

- 뜻: 보이지 않는
- 예문: The tiny scratch on the glass was almost invisible in daylight.

## 2485. whose

- 뜻: ~의, 누구의
- 예문: Whose jacket is hanging on the chair near the door?

## 2486. driving

- 뜻: 운전; 추진하는
- 예문: Heavy rain made driving on the mountain road quite dangerous.

## 2487. corporation

- 뜻: 주식회사, 대기업
- 예문: The large corporation opened a new office downtown last month.

## 2488. favourite

- 뜻: 가장 좋아하는
- 예문: Chocolate cake is her favourite dessert after a long dinner.

## 2489. senate

- 뜻: 상원
- 예문: The senate will vote on the new education bill tomorrow.

## 2490. boost

- 뜻: 끌어올리다, 증진하다; 상승
- 예문: A good night's sleep can boost your energy the next day.

## 2491. bias

- 뜻: 편견, 편향
- 예문: The article showed a clear bias against the smaller company.

## 2492. based

- 뜻: ~에 기반한, ~에 본사를 둔
- 예문: The film is based on a true story from her childhood.

## 2493. intervention

- 뜻: 개입, 중재
- 예문: Early intervention can help children who struggle with reading.

## 2494. prediction

- 뜻: 예측, 예보
- 예문: His prediction about the election turned out to be wrong.

## 2495. participant

- 뜻: 참가자
- 예문: Each participant received a badge at the start of the workshop.

## 2496. centre

- 뜻: 중심, 센터
- 예문: The shopping centre is closed on Monday for repairs.

## 2497. realise

- 뜻: 깨닫다, 실현하다
- 예문: I did not realise how late it was until the clock struck ten.

## 2498. monster

- 뜻: 괴물
- 예문: The little boy hid under the bed from the movie monster.

## 2499. presidential

- 뜻: 대통령의
- 예문: The presidential debate will air on television next Tuesday night.

## 2500. chronic

- 뜻: 만성적인, 오래 지속되는
- 예문: She has lived with chronic back pain for several years.
