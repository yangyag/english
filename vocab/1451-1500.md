# 필수 영어단어 1451–1500

## 1451. actual

- 뜻: 실제의, 사실상의
- 예문: The actual cost of the trip was higher than expected.

## 1452. function

- 뜻: 기능, 역할; 작동하다
- 예문: The main function of this button is to start the machine.

## 1453. active

- 뜻: 활동적인, 적극적인
- 예문: She stays active by walking around the park every morning.

## 1454. central

- 뜻: 중심의, 중앙의
- 예문: The train station is in the central part of town.

## 1455. soldier

- 뜻: 군인, 병사
- 예문: The young soldier wrote a letter home every week.

## 1456. diet

- 뜻: 식단, 식이요법
- 예문: A balanced diet helps you stay healthy during busy weeks.

## 1457. creative

- 뜻: 창의적인, 독창적인
- 예문: The children found a creative way to reuse old boxes.

## 1458. attract

- 뜻: 끌어당기다, 매혹하다
- 예문: Bright lights attract insects to the porch at night.

## 1459. foreign

- 뜻: 외국의
- 예문: She is studying a foreign language at the community college.

## 1460. belong

- 뜻: 속하다, ~의 것이다
- 예문: These keys belong to my neighbor across the hall.

## 1461. staff

- 뜻: 직원, 스태프
- 예문: The hotel staff helped us carry our bags upstairs.

## 1462. crisis

- 뜻: 위기
- 예문: The city faced a water crisis after the long drought.

## 1463. flight

- 뜻: 비행, 항공편
- 예문: Our flight to London leaves at six in the morning.

## 1464. recognize

- 뜻: 알아보다, 인정하다
- 예문: I did not recognize her with the new short haircut.

## 1465. digital

- 뜻: 디지털의
- 예문: Many students now take notes on a digital tablet.

## 1466. status

- 뜻: 지위, 상태
- 예문: Please check the status of your order on the website.

## 1467. entire

- 뜻: 전체의, 온
- 예문: We spent the entire afternoon cleaning the garage together.

## 1468. pleasure

- 뜻: 기쁨, 즐거움
- 예문: It was a pleasure to meet your family last night.

## 1469. desire

- 뜻: 욕구, 바라다
- 예문: Many people desire a quieter life away from the city.

## 1470. title

- 뜻: 제목, 직함
- 예문: The title of her new book appears on the cover.

## 1471. therapy

- 뜻: 치료, 요법
- 예문: Physical therapy helped him walk again after the accident.

## 1472. deliver

- 뜻: 배달하다, 전달하다
- 예문: The driver will deliver the package before noon today.

## 1473. familiar

- 뜻: 익숙한, 친숙한
- 예문: That song sounds familiar, but I cannot remember the name.

## 1474. solve

- 뜻: 해결하다, 풀다
- 예문: We need more time to solve this math problem.

## 1475. difficulty

- 뜻: 어려움, 곤란
- 예문: She had difficulty finding a parking space near work.

## 1476. stock

- 뜻: 재고, 주식
- 예문: The store is low on stock of bottled water today.

## 1477. agent

- 뜻: 대리인, 요원
- 예문: Our travel agent booked a cheaper hotel near the station.

## 1478. various

- 뜻: 다양한, 여러 가지의
- 예문: The museum displays various tools from the last century.

## 1479. target

- 뜻: 목표, 대상
- 예문: Our target is to finish the report by Friday.

## 1480. spirit

- 뜻: 정신, 기운
- 예문: The team showed great spirit after a difficult first half.

## 1481. estimate

- 뜻: 추정하다, 견적
- 예문: Can you estimate how long the repair will take?

## 1482. moral

- 뜻: 도덕의, 교훈
- 예문: The story has a simple moral about telling the truth.

## 1483. magazine

- 뜻: 잡지
- 예문: She reads a travel magazine on the train each morning.

## 1484. opposite

- 뜻: 반대의, 맞은편의
- 예문: The bank is on the opposite side of the street.

## 1485. following

- 뜻: 다음의, 다음에 오는
- 예문: Please read the following instructions before you start the test.

## 1486. obvious

- 뜻: 분명한, 명백한
- 예문: It was obvious that he had forgotten his house keys.

## 1487. upon

- 뜻: ~위에; ~하자마자
- 예문: Upon opening the box, we found a handwritten note.

## 1488. clothes

- 뜻: 옷, 의류
- 예문: He packed warm clothes for the trip to the mountains.

## 1489. alcohol

- 뜻: 알코올, 술
- 예문: The doctor asked him to avoid alcohol for two weeks.

## 1490. direct

- 뜻: 직접의; 감독하다
- 예문: Please give me a direct answer to this simple question.

## 1491. lay

- 뜻: 놓다, 눕히다
- 예문: Please lay the map on the table so we can see.

## 1492. background

- 뜻: 배경, 경력
- 예문: The mountains in the background look blue in the photo.

## 1493. guide

- 뜻: 안내하다, 안내자
- 예문: A local guide showed us the oldest streets in town.

## 1494. colleague

- 뜻: 동료
- 예문: My colleague offered to finish the report after lunch.

## 1495. commercial

- 뜻: 상업의, 광고
- 예문: That funny commercial made me remember the new product.

## 1496. terrible

- 뜻: 끔찍한, 심한
- 예문: We had terrible weather during our weekend at the lake.

## 1497. slightly

- 뜻: 약간, 조금
- 예문: The soup tasted slightly salty, so I added more water.

## 1498. personality

- 뜻: 성격, 개성

## 1499. importance

- 뜻: 중요성

## 1500. impossible

- 뜻: 불가능한
- 예문: It is impossible to finish this work in one hour.
