# 필수 영어단어 1551–1600

## 1551. typical

- 뜻: 전형적인, 보통의
- 예문: A typical weekday starts with coffee and a quick breakfast at home.

## 1552. soil

- 뜻: 흙, 토양
- 예문: The plants grew well in the rich soil of the garden.

## 1553. enter

- 뜻: 들어가다, 입력하다
- 예문: Please enter your password before you open the document.

## 1554. attempt

- 뜻: 시도하다, 시도
- 예문: She will attempt the exam again after more practice this week.

## 1555. afford

- 뜻: 여유가 있다, 감당할 수 있다
- 예문: I cannot afford a new car until I save more money.

## 1556. unfortunately

- 뜻: 불행히도, 유감스럽게도
- 예문: Unfortunately, the train was delayed because of heavy rain.

## 1557. beauty

- 뜻: 아름다움, 미
- 예문: The beauty of the sunset made everyone stop and watch.

## 1558. comment

- 뜻: 의견, 댓글
- 예문: He left a kind comment under her photo on social media.

## 1559. generate

- 뜻: 생성하다, 발생시키다
- 예문: Solar panels generate electricity even on cloudy winter days.

## 1560. disappear

- 뜻: 사라지다, 없어지다
- 예문: The sun will disappear behind the clouds in a few minutes.

## 1561. maintain

- 뜻: 유지하다, 관리하다
- 예문: You must maintain a steady pace if you want to finish.

## 1562. lawyer

- 뜻: 변호사
- 예문: My lawyer reviewed the contract before I signed it yesterday.

## 1563. possibly

- 뜻: 아마도, 어쩌면
- 예문: We could possibly arrive before noon if traffic stays light.

## 1564. ahead

- 뜻: 앞에, 앞으로
- 예문: There is a long line of cars ahead on the highway.

## 1565. ignore

- 뜻: 무시하다
- 예문: Please ignore the loud noise from the street during the meeting.

## 1566. anybody

- 뜻: 누구든지, 누군가
- 예문: Did anybody remember to lock the office door last night?

## 1567. everywhere

- 뜻: 어디에나, 도처에
- 예문: I looked everywhere for my keys but could not find them.

## 1568. progress

- 뜻: 진전, 발전
- 예문: The team made good progress on the project this week.

## 1569. thinking

- 뜻: 생각, 사고
- 예문: Clear thinking helps you make better decisions under pressure.

## 1570. habit

- 뜻: 습관
- 예문: Drinking water every morning is a healthy habit to keep.

## 1571. complain

- 뜻: 불평하다, 항의하다
- 예문: Customers complain when the food takes too long to arrive.

## 1572. celebrate

- 뜻: 축하하다, 기념하다
- 예문: We will celebrate her birthday with cake and music tonight.

## 1573. press

- 뜻: 누르다, 언론
- 예문: Press the green button to start the machine after checking.

## 1574. conflict

- 뜻: 갈등, 충돌
- 예문: The two teams had a conflict over the new practice schedule.

## 1575. convince

- 뜻: 설득하다, 납득시키다
- 예문: I tried to convince him to join us for dinner tonight.

## 1576. sale

- 뜻: 판매, 세일
- 예문: The store is having a big sale on winter coats this weekend.

## 1577. atmosphere

- 뜻: 분위기, 대기
- 예문: The restaurant has a warm atmosphere that makes guests feel welcome.

## 1578. criminal

- 뜻: 범죄자, 범죄의
- 예문: The police caught the criminal after a short chase downtown.

## 1579. substance

- 뜻: 물질, 실체
- 예문: This cleaning substance removes grease from kitchen floors quickly.

## 1580. shift

- 뜻: 옮기다, 교대, 전환
- 예문: She works the night shift at the hospital three times a week.

## 1581. writing

- 뜻: 글쓰기, 글
- 예문: His writing is clear and easy for beginners to understand.

## 1582. due

- 뜻: 기한이 된, ~때문에
- 예문: The homework is due on Friday before class begins.

## 1583. museum

- 뜻: 박물관
- 예문: We spent the afternoon at the art museum near the park.

## 1584. tour

- 뜻: 여행, 관광
- 예문: The guide led a walking tour of the old city center.

## 1585. separate

- 뜻: 분리하다, 별도의
- 예문: Please keep the wet clothes separate from the dry ones.

## 1586. intend

- 뜻: 의도하다, 작정하다
- 예문: I intend to finish this report before I leave work.

## 1587. destroy

- 뜻: 파괴하다
- 예문: The storm will destroy the old shed if we do not repair it.

## 1588. plenty

- 뜻: 충분한 양, 많음
- 예문: There is plenty of food left for everyone at the party.

## 1589. minister

- 뜻: 장관, 목사
- 예문: The health minister spoke about new hospital plans this morning.

## 1590. mass

- 뜻: 대량, 질량, 덩어리
- 예문: A large mass of ice broke away from the glacier last night.

## 1591. prime

- 뜻: 주요한, 최고의
- 예문: This is a prime location for a small cafe near campus.

## 1592. confidence

- 뜻: 자신감, 신뢰
- 예문: She spoke with confidence during her first job interview yesterday.

## 1593. article

- 뜻: 기사, 물품
- 예문: I read an interesting article about sleep in the newspaper.

## 1594. refuse

- 뜻: 거절하다
- 예문: They refuse to pay the extra fee without a clear reason.

## 1595. bone

- 뜻: 뼈
- 예문: The dog buried a bone under the tree in the backyard.

## 1596. announce

- 뜻: 발표하다, 알리다
- 예문: The principal will announce the winner after lunch today.

## 1597. murder

- 뜻: 살인, 살해하다
- 예문: The detective is investigating a murder that happened last winter.

## 1598. ring

- 뜻: 반지, 울리다
- 예문: She wore a silver ring on her left hand at dinner.

## 1599. breath

- 뜻: 숨, 호흡
- 예문: Take a deep breath before you begin your speech on stage.

## 1600. root

- 뜻: 뿌리, 근원
- 예문: The tree root grew under the sidewalk and lifted the stones.
