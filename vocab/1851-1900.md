# 필수 영어단어 1851–1900

## 1851. assess

- 뜻: 평가하다, 사정하다
- 예문: The teacher will assess your project at the end of the week.

## 1852. witness

- 뜻: 목격자, 목격하다
- 예문: A witness told the police what happened after the crash.

## 1853. emphasis

- 뜻: 강조, 중점
- 예문: The coach put special emphasis on teamwork during practice.

## 1854. designer

- 뜻: 디자이너, 설계자
- 예문: The designer showed us three new ideas for the website.

## 1855. visual

- 뜻: 시각의, 눈에 보이는
- 예문: The teacher used visual aids to explain the science lesson.

## 1856. relative

- 뜻: 친척, 상대적인
- 예문: A close relative will stay with us for the weekend.

## 1857. proud

- 뜻: 자랑스러운, 자부심이 있는
- 예문: She felt proud when her daughter won the science prize.

## 1858. plus

- 뜻: 더하기, 게다가, 이점
- 예문: The ticket costs twenty dollars plus tax at the door.

## 1859. extraordinary

- 뜻: 비범한, 놀라운
- 예문: She showed extraordinary skill during the final tennis match.

## 1860. entirely

- 뜻: 완전히, 전적으로
- 예문: The decision is entirely up to you and your family.

## 1861. wheel

- 뜻: 바퀴, 핸들
- 예문: One wheel of the bicycle was broken after the fall.

## 1862. complicated

- 뜻: 복잡한
- 예문: The instructions were too complicated for new users today.

## 1863. cash

- 뜻: 현금
- 예문: Please pay in cash because the card machine is broken.

## 1864. appreciate

- 뜻: 고마워하다, 진가를 알아보다
- 예문: I really appreciate your help with the heavy boxes.

## 1865. hardly

- 뜻: 거의 ~않다, 겨우
- 예문: I could hardly hear her voice in the noisy room.

## 1866. reputation

- 뜻: 평판, 명성
- 예문: The restaurant has a good reputation for fresh seafood.

## 1867. imagination

- 뜻: 상상력, 상상
- 예문: Children use their imagination when they invent new games.

## 1868. hero

- 뜻: 영웅, 주인공
- 예문: The firefighter became a hero after saving the family.

## 1869. wealth

- 뜻: 부, 재산
- 예문: He used his wealth to build schools in poor towns.

## 1870. suggestion

- 뜻: 제안, 의견
- 예문: Do you have a suggestion for a cheaper hotel nearby?

## 1871. seed

- 뜻: 씨앗
- 예문: Each seed needs water and sunlight to grow well.

## 1872. perfectly

- 뜻: 완벽하게, 아주
- 예문: The new shoes fit perfectly after I tried a smaller size.

## 1873. capacity

- 뜻: 용량, 능력
- 예문: This hall has a capacity of five hundred people.

## 1874. description

- 뜻: 설명, 묘사
- 예문: Please write a short description of your favorite movie.

## 1875. wedding

- 뜻: 결혼식
- 예문: We are going to their wedding on Saturday afternoon.

## 1876. display

- 뜻: 전시하다, 보여 주다, 전시
- 예문: The museum will display old maps from the last century.

## 1877. inform

- 뜻: 알리다, 통지하다
- 예문: Please inform me if the meeting time changes tomorrow.

## 1878. barrier

- 뜻: 장벽, 장애물
- 예문: Language can be a barrier when you travel abroad.

## 1879. employer

- 뜻: 고용주
- 예문: My employer offered me a better job in another city.

## 1880. saving

- 뜻: 저축, 절약
- 예문: Regular saving helped her buy a car last year.

## 1881. tongue

- 뜻: 혀, 언어
- 예문: He burned his tongue on the hot soup at lunch.

## 1882. revolution

- 뜻: 혁명, 대변혁
- 예문: The invention of the internet started a huge revolution.

## 1883. bet

- 뜻: 내기하다, 장담하다, 내기
- 예문: I bet it will rain before we finish the game.

## 1884. selection

- 뜻: 선택, 선발
- 예문: The store has a wide selection of winter coats this year.

## 1885. crash

- 뜻: 충돌, 충돌하다, 추락하다
- 예문: The car crash blocked the road for almost two hours.

## 1886. proof

- 뜻: 증거, 증명
- 예문: She had no proof that he took the missing keys.

## 1887. exciting

- 뜻: 신나는, 흥미진진한
- 예문: The last minutes of the game were really exciting.

## 1888. unlikely

- 뜻: 있을 것 같지 않은
- 예문: It is unlikely that we will finish before dinner tonight.

## 1889. immediate

- 뜻: 즉각적인, 당장의
- 예문: The problem needs immediate attention from the whole team.

## 1890. sharp

- 뜻: 날카로운, 예리한
- 예문: Be careful with that sharp knife in the kitchen drawer.

## 1891. winner

- 뜻: 우승자, 승자
- 예문: The winner of the contest received a trip to Paris.

## 1892. episode

- 뜻: (연속물의) 한 회, 사건
- 예문: We watched the last episode of the series last night.

## 1893. bury

- 뜻: 묻다, 매장하다
- 예문: The dog tried to bury the bone in the backyard.

## 1894. competitive

- 뜻: 경쟁적인, 경쟁력 있는
- 예문: She is very competitive and hates to lose any game.

## 1895. intention

- 뜻: 의도, 계획
- 예문: I have no intention of leaving this job next year.

## 1896. route

- 뜻: 경로, 노선, 길
- 예문: This bus route goes past the hospital and the station.

## 1897. freeze

- 뜻: 얼다, 얼리다
- 예문: Please freeze the leftover soup so we can eat it later.

## 1898. cigarette

- 뜻: 담배, 궐련
- 예문: He put out the cigarette before entering the building.

## 1899. vast

- 뜻: 광대한, 막대한
- 예문: They drove across a vast desert for most of the day.

## 1900. pregnant

- 뜻: 임신한
- 예문: She told her friends that she was pregnant last week.
