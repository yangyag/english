# 필수 영어단어 1951–2000

## 1951. shelf

- 뜻: 선반
- 예문: Please put the books back on the shelf after reading.

## 1952. singer

- 뜻: 가수
- 예문: The singer practiced for hours before the concert began.

## 1953. partly

- 뜻: 부분적으로, 어느 정도
- 예문: The delay was partly caused by heavy traffic this morning.

## 1954. distribute

- 뜻: 나누어 주다, 배포하다
- 예문: Volunteers will distribute food and water after the storm.

## 1955. might

- 뜻: ~일지도 모른다, 아마
- 예문: We might go to the park if the rain stops.

## 1956. whatever

- 뜻: 무엇이든, 어떤 ~이든
- 예문: You can order whatever you want from the lunch menu.

## 1957. fit

- 뜻: 맞다, 적합하다; 맞추다
- 예문: These shoes fit well and feel comfortable for walking.

## 1958. major

- 뜻: 주요한, 중대한; 전공
- 예문: Traffic is a major problem in this growing city.

## 1959. content

- 뜻: 내용, 콘텐츠; 만족한
- 예문: Check the content of the email before you send it.

## 1960. terms

- 뜻: 조건, 용어
- 예문: Read the terms of the contract before you sign anything.

## 1961. total

- 뜻: 전체의, 합계
- 예문: The total cost of the trip includes food and hotels.

## 1962. mission

- 뜻: 임무, 사명
- 예문: Their mission is to deliver supplies to remote villages.

## 1963. federal

- 뜻: 연방의
- 예문: The federal government announced new rules for public schools.

## 1964. somehow

- 뜻: 어떻게든, 왠지
- 예문: We somehow finished the project before the deadline arrived.

## 1965. firm

- 뜻: 회사, 기업; 단단한, 확고한
- 예문: She joined a law firm after finishing graduate school.

## 1966. novel

- 뜻: 소설; 새로운
- 예문: He stayed up late to finish the novel last night.

## 1967. Bond

- 뜻: 유대, 결속; 채권
- 예문: The Bond between the two friends grew stronger every year.

## 1968. gene

- 뜻: 유전자
- 예문: Scientists study how each gene affects a person's health.

## 1969. anxiety

- 뜻: 불안, 걱정
- 예문: Deep breathing can reduce anxiety before an important test.

## 1970. psychological

- 뜻: 심리적인, 정신적인
- 예문: Stress can have serious psychological effects on young workers.

## 1971. depression

- 뜻: 우울증; 불황, 침체
- 예문: She sought help after months of depression and poor sleep.

## 1972. set

- 뜻: 놓다, 정하다; 세트
- 예문: Please set the table before our guests arrive tonight.

## 1973. medium

- 뜻: 중간; 매체
- 예문: I ordered a medium coffee and a sandwich for lunch.

## 1974. resolution

- 뜻: 결심, 결의; 해결
- 예문: Her New Year resolution was to walk every morning.

## 1975. inspire

- 뜻: 영감을 주다, 고무하다
- 예문: Good teachers inspire students to ask better questions in class.

## 1976. gender

- 뜻: 성별
- 예문: The form asks you to write your name and gender.

## 1977. perception

- 뜻: 인식, 지각
- 예문: His perception of the problem changed after talking with her.

## 1978. essentially

- 뜻: 본질적으로, 기본적으로
- 예문: The two plans are essentially the same with different names.

## 1979. executive

- 뜻: 임원, 경영진; 행정의
- 예문: The company executive met with staff about the new policy.

## 1980. bite

- 뜻: 물다; 한입
- 예문: Don't bite the apple until I wash it first.

## 1981. interaction

- 뜻: 상호 작용, 교류
- 예문: Daily interaction with classmates helped her learn English faster.

## 1982. clever

- 뜻: 영리한, 똑똑한
- 예문: That was a clever way to solve a difficult puzzle.

## 1983. establish

- 뜻: 설립하다, 확립하다
- 예문: They hope to establish a new clinic in the town.

## 1984. trick

- 뜻: 속임수, 재주; 속이다
- 예문: He used a simple trick to remember the long number.

## 1985. abuse

- 뜻: 학대, 남용; 학대하다
- 예문: The report called for stronger laws against child abuse.

## 1986. program

- 뜻: 프로그램, 계획
- 예문: The school started a new program to support young readers.

## 1987. literally

- 뜻: 말 그대로, 문자 그대로
- 예문: The room was literally full of boxes after we moved.

## 1988. emission

- 뜻: 배출, 방출
- 예문: The factory reduced emission of smoke into the local air.

## 1989. romantic

- 뜻: 낭만적인, 연애의
- 예문: They planned a romantic dinner by the river at sunset.

## 1990. analysis

- 뜻: 분석
- 예문: Her analysis of the data helped the team make decisions.

## 1991. gas

- 뜻: 가스, 기체; 휘발유
- 예문: Please open a window if you smell gas in the kitchen.

## 1992. invent

- 뜻: 발명하다
- 예문: Scientists hope to invent a cheaper way to store energy.

## 1993. psychologist

- 뜻: 심리학자
- 예문: The psychologist listened carefully and then offered simple advice.

## 1994. majority

- 뜻: 대다수, 과반수
- 예문: A majority of voters supported the plan for new parks.

## 1995. related

- 뜻: 관련된, 친척의
- 예문: These two problems are related and should be solved together.

## 1996. solar

- 뜻: 태양의, 태양광의
- 예문: The family installed solar panels on their roof last summer.

## 1997. shake

- 뜻: 흔들다, 악수하다
- 예문: Please shake the bottle well before you open it.

## 1998. candidate

- 뜻: 후보자, 지원자
- 예문: Each candidate answered questions about jobs and education policy.

## 1999. survival

- 뜻: 생존, 살아남음
- 예문: Clean water is essential for survival after a natural disaster.

## 2000. historian

- 뜻: 역사가
- 예문: The historian spent years studying letters from the war.
