# 필수 영어단어 851–900

## 851. six

- 뜻: 여섯, 여섯 개의
- 예문: We need six chairs for the dinner table tonight.

## 852. explain

- 뜻: 설명하다
- 예문: Can you explain this math problem to me slowly?

## 853. research

- 뜻: 연구, 조사하다
- 예문: She is doing research on climate change at the university.

## 854. during

- 뜻: ~동안, ~중에
- 예문: Please keep your phone silent during the meeting.

## 855. able

- 뜻: ~할 수 있는
- 예문: I was able to finish the report before lunch.

## 856. both

- 뜻: 둘 다, 양쪽의
- 예문: Both of my parents work at the same hospital.

## 857. include

- 뜻: 포함하다
- 예문: Please include your email address on the application form.

## 858. finally

- 뜻: 마침내, 마지막으로
- 예문: We finally found a parking space near the station.

## 859. describe

- 뜻: 묘사하다, 설명하다
- 예문: Can you describe the man who took your bag?

## 860. since

- 뜻: ~이래로; ~때문에
- 예문: I have lived here since I finished high school.

## 861. level

- 뜻: 수준, 레벨; 높이
- 예문: This course is for students at an intermediate level.

## 862. history

- 뜻: 역사, 이력
- 예문: She is writing a book about the history of jazz.

## 863. sense

- 뜻: 감각, 의미; 느끼다
- 예문: His story did not make any sense to me.

## 864. allow

- 뜻: 허락하다, 허용하다
- 예문: The teacher will not allow phones in the classroom.

## 865. anything

- 뜻: 무엇이든, 아무것도
- 예문: Call me if you need anything from the store.

## 866. public

- 뜻: 공공의, 대중의; 대중
- 예문: The park is open to the public every day.

## 867. against

- 뜻: ~에 반대하여, ~에 맞서
- 예문: Many people spoke against the new parking rules.

## 868. sometimes

- 뜻: 가끔, 때때로
- 예문: I sometimes walk to work when the weather is nice.

## 869. control

- 뜻: 통제하다; 통제, 조종
- 예문: Please try to control your voice in the library.

## 870. probably

- 뜻: 아마, 아마도
- 예문: She will probably arrive at the station after lunch.

## 871. situation

- 뜻: 상황, 처지
- 예문: This is a difficult situation for the whole family.

## 872. quite

- 뜻: 꽤, 상당히; 완전히
- 예문: The test was quite easy once I started studying.

## 873. result

- 뜻: 결과; 결과로 생기다
- 예문: We will get the test result by Friday afternoon.

## 874. else

- 뜻: 그 밖의, 다른
- 예문: Would you like anything else with your coffee?

## 875. process

- 뜻: 과정, 절차; 처리하다
- 예문: Learning a language is a slow process for most people.

## 876. mind

- 뜻: 마음, 정신; 꺼리다
- 예문: Do you mind if I open the window a little?

## 877. everyone

- 뜻: 모두, 모든 사람
- 예문: Everyone in the class passed the final exam.

## 878. develop

- 뜻: 발전하다, 개발하다
- 예문: The company plans to develop a new phone next year.

## 879. according

- 뜻: ~에 따르면 (according to)
- 예문: According to the weather report, it will snow tonight.

## 880. effect

- 뜻: 효과, 영향
- 예문: This medicine had a strong effect on my headache.

## 881. second

- 뜻: 두 번째의; 초
- 예문: This is the second time I have visited this city.

## 882. everything

- 뜻: 모든 것, 전부
- 예문: Everything in this room belongs to my older sister.

## 883. four

- 뜻: 넷, 네 개의
- 예문: There are four people waiting outside the office.

## 884. cause

- 뜻: 원인; 일으키다
- 예문: Heavy rain was the main cause of the flood.

## 885. nothing

- 뜻: 아무것도 없음, 아무것도
- 예문: There is nothing left in the refrigerator tonight.

## 886. difficult

- 뜻: 어려운
- 예문: This puzzle is too difficult for young children.

## 887. five

- 뜻: 다섯, 다섯 개의
- 예문: She starts work at five o'clock every morning.

## 888. almost

- 뜻: 거의
- 예문: I almost missed the last bus home last night.

## 889. phone

- 뜻: 전화, 휴대전화
- 예문: Please put your phone away during the movie.

## 890. type

- 뜻: 종류, 유형; 타자하다
- 예문: What type of music do you usually listen to?

## 891. although

- 뜻: 비록 ~이지만
- 예문: Although it was raining, we still went for a walk.

## 892. society

- 뜻: 사회; 협회
- 예문: Modern society depends on computers more than ever.

## 893. language

- 뜻: 언어, 말
- 예문: English is a useful language for travel and work.

## 894. technology

- 뜻: 기술, 과학 기술
- 예문: New technology has changed the way we work.

## 895. moment

- 뜻: 순간, 잠깐
- 예문: Please wait a moment while I check the schedule.

## 896. add

- 뜻: 더하다, 추가하다
- 예문: Please add your name to the list on the board.

## 897. OK

- 뜻: 괜찮은, 좋다; 알겠다
- 예문: Is it OK if we meet at the cafe after class?

## 898. relationship

- 뜻: 관계, 인간관계
- 예문: She has a close relationship with her younger brother.

## 899. community

- 뜻: 공동체, 지역 사회
- 예문: Our community built a new playground for the children.

## 900. already

- 뜻: 이미, 벌써
- 예문: I have already finished my homework for tonight.
