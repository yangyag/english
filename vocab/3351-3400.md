# 필수 영어단어 3351–3400

## 3351. prior

- 뜻: 이전의, 사전의; 우선하는
- 예문: She had no prior experience with this kind of work.

## 3352. surrounding

- 뜻: 주변의, 주위의
- 예문: The surrounding hills looked beautiful in the early morning light.

## 3353. partnership

- 뜻: 협력, 제휴, 동업
- 예문: The two companies formed a partnership to share research costs.

## 3354. fate

- 뜻: 운명, 숙명
- 예문: He believed fate had brought them together that day.

## 3355. mere

- 뜻: 단순한, 겨우 ~에 불과한
- 예문: It was a mere mistake, not a serious crime.

## 3356. slight

- 뜻: 약간의, 가벼운
- 예문: There was a slight delay before the train finally arrived.

## 3357. mount

- 뜻: 설치하다, 오르다; 증가하다
- 예문: We need to mount this shelf on the kitchen wall.

## 3358. angle

- 뜻: 각도, 각; 관점
- 예문: He took the photo from a different angle this time.

## 3359. blank

- 뜻: 빈, 공백의
- 예문: Please leave this line blank if you have no comments.

## 3360. contest

- 뜻: 대회, 경연; 이의를 제기하다
- 예문: She won first prize in the school writing contest last week.

## 3361. examination

- 뜻: 시험, 검사
- 예문: The doctor completed a careful examination of her injured knee.

## 3362. photographer

- 뜻: 사진작가, 사진사
- 예문: The photographer asked us to smile for the family picture.

## 3363. detailed

- 뜻: 자세한, 상세한
- 예문: Please send a detailed report of the meeting by Friday.

## 3364. summary

- 뜻: 요약, 개요
- 예문: The manager asked for a short summary of the discussion.

## 3365. beg

- 뜻: 간청하다, 구걸하다
- 예문: The children beg their parents to stay a little longer.

## 3366. export

- 뜻: 수출하다; 수출
- 예문: The country will export more rice to nearby markets this year.

## 3367. snake

- 뜻: 뱀
- 예문: A snake moved slowly across the path in the grass.

## 3368. injure

- 뜻: 다치게 하다, 부상입히다
- 예문: Be careful not to injure yourself while lifting those boxes.

## 3369. troop

- 뜻: 군대, 부대; 무리
- 예문: A troop of scouts hiked along the mountain trail together.

## 3370. opposed

- 뜻: 반대하는
- 예문: Many local residents are opposed to the new highway project.

## 3371. depressed

- 뜻: 우울한, 침체된
- 예문: She felt depressed after she lost her job last month.

## 3372. regime

- 뜻: 정권, 체제
- 예문: The new regime promised major reforms after years of conflict.

## 3373. stir

- 뜻: 휘젓다, 일으키다; 동요
- 예문: Please stir the soup slowly so it does not burn.

## 3374. carrot

- 뜻: 당근
- 예문: She chopped a carrot and added it to the soup.

## 3375. neat

- 뜻: 단정한, 깔끔한
- 예문: Keep your room neat before the guests arrive this evening.

## 3376. bride

- 뜻: 신부
- 예문: The bride walked down the aisle with a bright smile.

## 3377. participation

- 뜻: 참여, 참가
- 예문: Active student participation in class discussions has improved this semester.

## 3378. investigator

- 뜻: 수사관, 조사원
- 예문: The investigator asked several questions about the missing office documents.

## 3379. independence

- 뜻: 독립
- 예문: The country celebrated its independence with a large public parade.

## 3380. interpretation

- 뜻: 해석, 통역
- 예문: Her interpretation of the poem was quite different from mine.

## 3381. confront

- 뜻: 맞서다, 직면하다
- 예문: We must confront the problem before it grows any worse.

## 3382. provision

- 뜻: 조항; 공급, 대비
- 예문: The contract includes a provision for extra payment after delays.

## 3383. burden

- 뜻: 부담, 짐
- 예문: Caring for two jobs became a heavy burden for him.

## 3384. cabinet

- 뜻: 수납장, 캐비닛; 내각
- 예문: Please put the clean glasses back in the kitchen cabinet.

## 3385. dispute

- 뜻: 분쟁, 논쟁
- 예문: The neighbors had a dispute over the fence between their yards.

## 3386. strategic

- 뜻: 전략적인
- 예문: They made a strategic decision to enter the Asian market.

## 3387. latter

- 뜻: 후자의, 후반의
- 예문: Between the two plans, we chose the latter for cost reasons.

## 3388. withdraw

- 뜻: 인출하다, 철수하다, 철회하다
- 예문: She went to the bank to withdraw some cash this morning.

## 3389. deck

- 뜻: 갑판; 데크, 마루
- 예문: We sat on the deck and watched the sunset together.

## 3390. offense

- 뜻: 위반, 범죄; 모욕; 공격
- 예문: Speeding is a traffic offense that can cost you money.

## 3391. extensive

- 뜻: 광범위한, 대규모의
- 예문: The storm caused extensive damage to homes across the coastal towns.

## 3392. stability

- 뜻: 안정, 안정성
- 예문: The new policy aims to bring economic stability to the country.

## 3393. chapter

- 뜻: (책의) 장; 지부
- 예문: Please read the next chapter before class on Monday morning.

## 3394. shade

- 뜻: 그늘; 색조
- 예문: We sat in the shade of a large oak tree.

## 3395. furthermore

- 뜻: 게다가, 더욱이
- 예문: The plan is cheaper; furthermore, it will save us time.

## 3396. tunnel

- 뜻: 터널
- 예문: The train passed through a long tunnel under the mountain.

## 3397. architecture

- 뜻: 건축, 건축학; 구조
- 예문: She studied architecture in college and now designs public buildings.

## 3398. departure

- 뜻: 출발, 떠남
- 예문: Our departure was delayed because of heavy rain this morning.

## 3399. cancel

- 뜻: 취소하다
- 예문: They had to cancel the picnic because of the storm.

## 3400. servant

- 뜻: 하인, 종
- 예문: The wealthy family employed a servant to cook and clean.
