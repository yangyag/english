# 필수 영어단어 1001–1050

## 1001. benefit

- 뜻: 이익, 혜택; 이롭다
- 예문: Regular exercise can benefit your health in many ways.

## 1002. movement

- 뜻: 움직임, 운동; 사회 운동
- 예문: The sudden movement of the car made everyone nervous.

## 1003. including

- 뜻: ~을 포함하여
- 예문: Everyone is coming to dinner, including my parents tonight.

## 1004. quality

- 뜻: 질, 품질; 자질
- 예문: This store is known for the quality of its products.

## 1005. yourself

- 뜻: 너 자신, 당신 자신
- 예문: You should take better care of yourself during busy weeks.

## 1006. similar

- 뜻: 비슷한, 유사한
- 예문: Their ideas are similar, but the details are quite different.

## 1007. anyone

- 뜻: 누구든지, 아무나
- 예문: Anyone can join the club if they fill out this form.

## 1008. offer

- 뜻: 제안하다, 제공하다; 제안
- 예문: They will offer her a better job next week.

## 1009. organisation

- 뜻: 조직, 단체; 구성
- 예문: She works for a large organisation that helps homeless people.

## 1010. voice

- 뜻: 목소리; 의견
- 예문: I recognized her voice as soon as she answered the phone.

## 1011. TV

- 뜻: 텔레비전
- 예문: We watched the news on TV after dinner last night.

## 1012. worry

- 뜻: 걱정하다, 걱정
- 예문: Don't worry about the test if you studied last night.

## 1013. rule

- 뜻: 규칙; 지배하다
- 예문: The first rule of the library is to keep quiet.

## 1014. improve

- 뜻: 향상시키다, 개선하다
- 예문: She practices every day to improve her English speaking skills.

## 1015. particularly

- 뜻: 특히, 특별히
- 예문: I like all fruit, but I particularly enjoy fresh strawberries.

## 1016. indeed

- 뜻: 정말로, 과연
- 예문: The movie was indeed better than the reviews had suggested.

## 1017. effort

- 뜻: 노력
- 예문: He put a lot of effort into finishing the project.

## 1018. per

- 뜻: ~당, ~마다
- 예문: The tickets cost twenty dollars per person for the concert.

## 1019. material

- 뜻: 재료, 물질; 자료
- 예문: We need more material to finish building the wooden table.

## 1020. check

- 뜻: 확인하다; 수표, 계산서
- 예문: Please check your email before you leave the office today.

## 1021. accept

- 뜻: 받아들이다, 수락하다
- 예문: She decided to accept the job offer from the company.

## 1022. author

- 뜻: 저자, 작가
- 예문: The author signed copies of her new book after the talk.

## 1023. simply

- 뜻: 단순히, 그저; 간단히
- 예문: You simply need to press this button to start the machine.

## 1024. third

- 뜻: 세 번째의; 3분의 1
- 예문: She finished third in the race and won a medal.

## 1025. simple

- 뜻: 간단한, 단순한
- 예문: The recipe is simple, so even beginners can cook it.

## 1026. brain

- 뜻: 뇌, 두뇌
- 예문: Sleep is important because it helps your brain rest and recover.

## 1027. relate

- 뜻: 관련시키다; 공감하다
- 예문: I can relate to her story because I had similar problems.

## 1028. item

- 뜻: 항목, 물건
- 예문: Please put each item back on the shelf after you look.

## 1029. expert

- 뜻: 전문가; 숙련된
- 예문: We asked an expert to check the roof after the storm.

## 1030. design

- 뜻: 설계하다, 디자인하다; 설계, 디자인
- 예문: They will design a new logo for the company website.

## 1031. memory

- 뜻: 기억, 추억; 메모리
- 예문: I have a clear memory of our first day at school.

## 1032. serious

- 뜻: 심각한, 진지한
- 예문: This is a serious problem that we need to solve quickly.

## 1033. physical

- 뜻: 신체의, 물리적인
- 예문: He does physical exercise every morning before he goes to work.

## 1034. instead

- 뜻: 대신에
- 예문: We stayed home instead of going out in the heavy rain.

## 1035. scientist

- 뜻: 과학자
- 예문: The scientist explained her research in simple words to the class.

## 1036. pressure

- 뜻: 압력, 압박
- 예문: He felt a lot of pressure before the important job interview.

## 1037. medical

- 뜻: 의학의, 의료의
- 예문: She needed medical help after she fell down the stairs.

## 1038. affect

- 뜻: 영향을 미치다
- 예문: Lack of sleep can affect your mood and your work.

## 1039. response

- 뜻: 응답, 반응
- 예문: We are still waiting for a response to our email.

## 1040. player

- 뜻: 선수, 연주자; 플레이어
- 예문: He is the best player on the school basketball team.

## 1041. member

- 뜻: 회원, 구성원
- 예문: She has been a member of the book club for years.

## 1042. development

- 뜻: 발전, 개발; 발달
- 예문: The city plans more development along the river next year.

## 1043. modern

- 뜻: 현대의, 최신의
- 예문: They live in a modern apartment near the train station.

## 1044. adult

- 뜻: 성인; 어른의
- 예문: You must be an adult to buy tickets for this movie.

## 1045. university

- 뜻: 대학교
- 예문: She hopes to study history at university after high school.

## 1046. decade

- 뜻: 10년, 십 년간
- 예문: The town has changed a lot over the past decade.

## 1047. nature

- 뜻: 자연; 본성
- 예문: We spent the weekend walking in nature near the lake.

## 1048. several

- 뜻: 몇몇의, 여러
- 예문: I have visited several countries in Europe over the years.

## 1049. factor

- 뜻: 요인, 요소
- 예문: Price is an important factor when people choose a new car.

## 1050. choice

- 뜻: 선택, 선택권
- 예문: You have a choice between tea and coffee this morning.
