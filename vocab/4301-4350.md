# 필수 영어단어 4301–4350

## 4301. harbour

- 뜻: 항구; 품다, 숨겨 두다
- 예문: The fishing boats returned to the harbour before the storm.

## 4302. prey

- 뜻: 먹이, 사냥감; 희생자
- 예문: The lion waited in the grass for its prey.

## 4303. persistent

- 뜻: 끈질긴, 집요한; 지속적인
- 예문: Her persistent effort finally helped her pass the exam.

## 4304. reassure

- 뜻: 안심시키다, 다시 확신시키다
- 예문: The doctor tried to reassure the worried parents.

## 4305. interruption

- 뜻: 중단, 방해
- 예문: Please wait until the meeting ends without interruption.

## 4306. hundredth

- 뜻: 100번째의; 100분의 1
- 예문: This is the hundredth time I have heard that joke.

## 4307. archive

- 뜻: 기록 보관소; 보관하다
- 예문: The museum will archive the old photographs next week.

## 4308. artistically

- 뜻: 예술적으로
- 예문: She arranged the flowers artistically on the dining table.

## 4309. affection

- 뜻: 애정, 호의
- 예문: The child showed great affection for her grandmother.

## 4310. alphabetical

- 뜻: 알파벳 순의
- 예문: Please list all the student names in alphabetical order.

## 4311. broadly

- 뜻: 대체로, 넓게
- 예문: Broadly speaking, the plan is a good idea.

## 4312. bureaucracy

- 뜻: 관료제, 관료주의
- 예문: The new rules created more bureaucracy in the office.

## 4313. wit

- 뜻: 재치, 기지
- 예문: His quick wit made everyone at the table laugh.

## 4314. pronunciation

- 뜻: 발음
- 예문: Her pronunciation of English has improved this year.

## 4315. eternal

- 뜻: 영원한, 끝없는
- 예문: They promised eternal love during the wedding ceremony.

## 4316. chemist

- 뜻: 약사, 약국; 화학자
- 예문: I went to the chemist to buy some cough medicine.

## 4317. timetable

- 뜻: 시간표
- 예문: Check the timetable before you leave for the station.

## 4318. unacceptable

- 뜻: 받아들일 수 없는, 용납할 수 없는
- 예문: That rude comment was completely unacceptable at work.

## 4319. useless

- 뜻: 쓸모없는, 무익한
- 예문: This broken phone is now completely useless to me.

## 4320. whereby

- 뜻: ~함으로써, ~에 의하여
- 예문: They signed a deal whereby both companies share profits.

## 4321. refusal

- 뜻: 거절, 거부
- 예문: Her refusal to join the team surprised everyone.

## 4322. renew

- 뜻: 갱신하다, 새롭게 하다
- 예문: You must renew your passport before you travel abroad.

## 4323. probable

- 뜻: 있을 법한, 개연성 있는
- 예문: Rain is probable this afternoon according to the forecast.

## 4324. pronounced

- 뜻: 뚜렷한, 현저한
- 예문: There is a pronounced difference between the two designs.

## 4325. pleasing

- 뜻: 기분 좋은, 마음에 드는
- 예문: The garden has a pleasing mix of colors and scents.

## 4326. certificate

- 뜻: 증명서, 자격증
- 예문: She received a certificate after completing the training course.

## 4327. exclusion

- 뜻: 제외, 배제
- 예문: The exclusion of late applications was clearly stated.

## 4328. substitute

- 뜻: 대체하다; 대용품, 대리인
- 예문: You can substitute honey for sugar in this recipe.

## 4329. hostility

- 뜻: 적대감, 적의
- 예문: There was open hostility between the two rival groups.

## 4330. morality

- 뜻: 도덕, 도덕성
- 예문: The novel raises difficult questions about morality and power.

## 4331. acre

- 뜻: 에이커(면적 단위)
- 예문: The farm covers one acre behind the old barn.

## 4332. diplomat

- 뜻: 외교관
- 예문: The diplomat worked hard to keep peace between nations.

## 4333. sometime

- 뜻: 언젠가, 조만간
- 예문: We should meet for coffee sometime next week.

## 4334. rugby

- 뜻: 럭비
- 예문: He plays rugby with his friends every Saturday morning.

## 4335. paragraph

- 뜻: 단락, 문단
- 예문: Please write a short paragraph about your summer holiday.

## 4336. teaspoon

- 뜻: 티스푼, 작은 숟가락
- 예문: Add one teaspoon of salt to the soup.

## 4337. infant

- 뜻: 유아, 아기
- 예문: The infant slept quietly in her mother's arms.

## 4338. formation

- 뜻: 형성, 구성; 대형
- 예문: The clouds moved in a long formation across the sky.

## 4339. scope

- 뜻: 범위, 여지
- 예문: The project is beyond the scope of our small team.

## 4340. handful

- 뜻: 한 줌; 소수
- 예문: Only a handful of students stayed after class.

## 4341. downtown

- 뜻: 도심, 시내
- 예문: We went downtown to see a movie last night.

## 4342. eager

- 뜻: 열망하는, 간절한
- 예문: The children were eager to open their birthday gifts.

## 4343. demonstration

- 뜻: 시연, 설명; 시위
- 예문: The teacher gave a demonstration of the new software.

## 4344. cluster

- 뜻: 무리, 군집; 모이다
- 예문: A cluster of shops stood near the train station.

## 4345. identification

- 뜻: 신원 확인, 신분증; 식별
- 예문: You must show identification before you enter the building.

## 4346. transformation

- 뜻: 변화, 변신, 변환
- 예문: The old factory underwent a complete transformation last year.

## 4347. nonetheless

- 뜻: 그럼에도 불구하고
- 예문: The hike was difficult; nonetheless, we reached the top.

## 4348. sacred

- 뜻: 신성한, 성스러운
- 예문: The mountain is considered sacred by the local people.

## 4349. homeless

- 뜻: 집 없는, 노숙자의
- 예문: The city opened a shelter for homeless families this winter.

## 4350. trunk

- 뜻: (나무) 줄기, 트렁크, 코끼리 코
- 예문: He packed his clothes into the trunk of the car.
