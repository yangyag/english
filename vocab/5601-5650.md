# 필수 영어단어 5601–5650

## 5601. graveyard

- 뜻: 묘지, 공동묘지
- 예문: We walked past the old graveyard on our way to church.

## 5602. inclination

- 뜻: 경향, 성향; 기울기
- 예문: She has a strong inclination to help people in need.

## 5603. incapable

- 뜻: 할 수 없는, 무능한
- 예문: He felt incapable of finishing the report before Friday.

## 5604. implore

- 뜻: 간청하다, 애원하다
- 예문: I implore you to wait until we have more information.

## 5605. horseman

- 뜻: 기수, 말을 탄 사람
- 예문: The horseman rode slowly along the dusty country road.

## 5606. humiliation

- 뜻: 굴욕, 창피
- 예문: Losing in front of his friends was a deep humiliation.

## 5607. iniquity

- 뜻: 사악함, 부당함
- 예문: The judge spoke against the iniquity of the unfair law.

## 5608. infringe

- 뜻: 침해하다, 위반하다
- 예문: Those new rules must not infringe on our basic rights.

## 5609. indignation

- 뜻: 분개, 의분
- 예문: She felt deep indignation when they ignored her honest complaint.

## 5610. propagate

- 뜻: 퍼뜨리다, 전파하다; 번식시키다
- 예문: False rumors can propagate quickly through social media at night.

## 5611. recede

- 뜻: 물러나다, 멀어지다
- 예문: The floodwaters began to recede after several days of rain.

## 5612. redeem

- 뜻: 되찾다, 만회하다; 구원하다
- 예문: She hoped extra work would redeem her earlier careless mistake.

## 5613. refund

- 뜻: 환불하다, 환불금
- 예문: The store will refund your money if the shoes do not fit.

## 5614. respectable

- 뜻: 존경받을 만한, 괜찮은
- 예문: He found a respectable job after years of odd work.

## 5615. reproach

- 뜻: 비난하다, 나무라다
- 예문: Her parents did not reproach her for coming home late.

## 5616. profess

- 뜻: 공언하다, 표명하다
- 예문: Many candidates profess support for education during every campaign.

## 5617. procession

- 뜻: 행렬, 행진
- 예문: A long procession of cars moved slowly toward the stadium.

## 5618. patty

- 뜻: 패티, 다진 고기 조각
- 예문: She placed a beef patty on the grill for dinner.

## 5619. mislead

- 뜻: 오도하다, 잘못 이끌다
- 예문: The advertisement may mislead shoppers about the true price.

## 5620. misunderstanding

- 뜻: 오해
- 예문: Their argument started from a simple misunderstanding about the schedule.

## 5621. Neptune

- 뜻: 해왕성
- 예문: Neptune is a distant planet far beyond the orbit of Uranus.

## 5622. mound

- 뜻: 언덕, 더미
- 예문: Children built a small mound of sand near the water.

## 5623. mortal

- 뜻: 죽을 운명의, 인간의; 치명적인
- 예문: Every mortal being must face the end of life someday.

## 5624. pare

- 뜻: 껍질을 깎다, 줄이다
- 예문: Please pare the apples before you slice them for pie.

## 5625. pact

- 뜻: 협정, 약속
- 예문: The two countries signed a pact to reduce trade barriers.

## 5626. debtor

- 뜻: 채무자
- 예문: The debtor promised to pay back the loan next month.

## 5627. conspire

- 뜻: 공모하다, 음모를 꾸미다
- 예문: Several workers conspired to hide the missing money from managers.

## 5628. consternation

- 뜻: 경악, 당혹
- 예문: The sudden power cut caused consternation throughout the packed theater.

## 5629. constituent

- 뜻: 구성 요소; 유권자
- 예문: Each constituent called the senator about the proposed tax bill.

## 5630. disgrace

- 뜻: 불명예, 수치
- 예문: Cheating on the exam brought disgrace to the whole team.

## 5631. disillusion

- 뜻: 환멸을 느끼게 하다
- 예문: Years of low pay began to disillusion the young teacher.

## 5632. diligent

- 뜻: 근면한, 성실한
- 예문: She is a diligent student who reviews her notes every night.

## 5633. dice

- 뜻: 주사위; 잘게 썰다
- 예문: He rolled the dice and hoped for a lucky number.

## 5634. derision

- 뜻: 조롱, 비웃음
- 예문: His unusual idea was met with derision from the whole class.

## 5635. dubious

- 뜻: 의심스러운, 미심쩍은
- 예문: I am dubious about his promise to finish everything tonight.

## 5636. diverge

- 뜻: 갈라지다, 분기하다
- 예문: The two hiking trails diverge just beyond the wooden bridge.

## 5637. duel

- 뜻: 결투
- 예문: The two rivals agreed to a duel at dawn in the field.

## 5638. eccentric

- 뜻: 괴짜의, 별난
- 예문: Our eccentric neighbor paints his fence a different color each month.

## 5639. foresee

- 뜻: 예견하다, 미리 알다
- 예문: Nobody could foresee the sudden change in the weather.

## 5640. fertilise

- 뜻: 비료를 주다, 수정시키다
- 예문: Farmers fertilise the fields before planting corn each spring.

## 5641. fertilize

- 뜻: 비료를 주다, 수정시키다
- 예문: Gardeners fertilize the soil so the roses grow more quickly.

## 5642. glitter

- 뜻: 반짝이다, 반짝임
- 예문: Stars glitter above the quiet lake on a clear night.

## 5643. fro

## 5650. fearful

- 뜻: 두려운, 겁내는
- 예문: The child was fearful of the dark hallway at night.
