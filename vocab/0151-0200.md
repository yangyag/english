# 필수 영어단어 151–200

## 151. million

- 뜻: 백만
- 예문: The company sold more than one million phones last year.

## 152. food

- 뜻: 음식
- 예문: We bought some fresh food at the market this morning.

## 153. every

- 뜻: 모든, 각각의
- 예문: She walks her dog every morning before she goes to work.

## 154. last

- 뜻: 마지막의, 지난; 지속하다
- 예문: I saw him last week at the grocery store.

## 155. name

- 뜻: 이름; 이름을 짓다
- 예문: Please write your name at the top of the page.

## 156. point

- 뜻: 점, 요점; 가리키다
- 예문: She made a good point about the new school rule.

## 157. water

- 뜻: 물
- 예문: Please drink more water when you exercise in summer.

## 158. school

- 뜻: 학교
- 예문: My children walk to school together every weekday morning.

## 159. young

- 뜻: 젊은, 어린
- 예문: The young couple just moved into the apartment next door.

## 160. bring

- 뜻: 가져오다, 데려오다
- 예문: Can you bring some snacks to the party tonight?

## 161. story

- 뜻: 이야기, 소설
- 예문: He told a funny story about his first day at work.

## 162. home

- 뜻: 집, 가정; 집으로
- 예문: We went home after dinner and watched a movie.

## 163. speak

- 뜻: 말하다, 이야기하다
- 예문: She can speak English well after years of practice.

## 164. body

- 뜻: 몸, 신체
- 예문: Regular exercise helps keep your body strong and healthy.

## 165. book

- 뜻: 책
- 예문: I borrowed a new book from the library yesterday.

## 166. away

- 뜻: 떨어져, 멀리
- 예문: They live far away from the city center now.

## 167. bad

- 뜻: 나쁜
- 예문: The food was bad, so we left the restaurant early.

## 168. week

- 뜻: 주, 일주일
- 예문: I work from home three days a week.

## 169. study

- 뜻: 공부하다, 연구하다
- 예문: She likes to study English in the quiet library.

## 170. leave

- 뜻: 떠나다; 남기다
- 예문: We have to leave the office at five o'clock.

## 171. hour

- 뜻: 시간, 한 시간
- 예문: It takes about an hour to drive to the airport.

## 172. write

- 뜻: 쓰다, 작성하다
- 예문: Please write your email address on this form.

## 173. large

- 뜻: 큰, 넓은
- 예문: They live in a large house near the river.

## 174. friend

- 뜻: 친구
- 예문: I met an old friend for coffee after work.

## 175. run

- 뜻: 달리다; 운영하다
- 예문: He likes to run in the park every morning.

## 176. stop

- 뜻: 멈추다, 그만두다
- 예문: Please stop talking while the teacher is speaking.

## 177. turn

- 뜻: 돌다, 돌리다; 차례
- 예문: Turn left at the next street and you will see it.

## 178. real

- 뜻: 실제의, 진짜의
- 예문: Is this a real diamond or just a copy?

## 179. hand

- 뜻: 손
- 예문: She raised her hand to ask a question in class.

## 180. remember

- 뜻: 기억하다
- 예문: I still remember the first day we met.

## 181. once

- 뜻: 한 번; 한때
- 예문: I once lived in a small town by the sea.

## 182. pay

- 뜻: 지불하다
- 예문: I need to pay the rent before Friday this week.

## 183. course

- 뜻: 과정, 강좌; 코스
- 예문: She is taking a cooking course at the community center.

## 184. city

- 뜻: 도시
- 예문: Many young people move to the city for work.

## 185. few

- 뜻: 적은 수의, 몇몇의
- 예문: Only a few students came to class on Friday.

## 186. understand

- 뜻: 이해하다
- 예문: I did not understand the last part of the lesson.

## 187. love

- 뜻: 사랑하다; 사랑
- 예문: I love spending time with my family on weekends.

## 188. grow

- 뜻: 자라다, 성장하다
- 예문: These plants grow well in a sunny window.

## 189. spend

- 뜻: (시간·돈을) 쓰다, 보내다
- 예문: We spend too much money on coffee every month.

## 190. head

- 뜻: 머리; 책임자
- 예문: He shook his head and said he did not agree.

## 191. game

- 뜻: 경기, 게임
- 예문: The kids played a board game after dinner.

## 192. case

- 뜻: 경우, 사례; 사건
- 예문: In this case, we should wait for more information.

## 193. early

- 뜻: 이른, 일찍
- 예문: I woke up early to finish my homework.

## 194. sure

- 뜻: 확실한, 확신하는
- 예문: Are you sure this is the right address?

## 195. read

- 뜻: 읽다
- 예문: She likes to read novels before she goes to sleep.

## 196. together

- 뜻: 함께
- 예문: Let's work together on this project after lunch.

## 197. hold

- 뜻: 잡다, 들다; 개최하다
- 예문: Please hold my bag while I look for my keys.

## 198. meet

- 뜻: 만나다
- 예문: Let's meet at the cafe near the station at noon.

## 199. interest

- 뜻: 관심, 흥미; 이자
- 예문: He has a strong interest in science and space.

## 200. buy

- 뜻: 사다, 구입하다
- 예문: I need to buy some milk on the way home.
