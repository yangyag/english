# 필수 영어단어 5151–5200

## 5151. inhabit

- 뜻: 살다, 거주하다
- 예문: Many rare birds inhabit the wetlands along this quiet river.

## 5152. shorten

- 뜻: 짧게 하다, 단축하다
- 예문: We should shorten the meeting so everyone can leave on time.

## 5153. uncommon

- 뜻: 드문, 흔하지 않은
- 예문: Snow in April is uncommon in this part of the country.

## 5154. birthplace

- 뜻: 출생지, 발상지

## 5155. advertiser

## 5156. disbelief

## 5157. captivity

## 5158. sleepy

## 5159. squash

- 뜻: 으깨다; 호박; 스쿼시

## 5160. inclusive

## 5161. compatible

## 5172. decree

## 5179. junk
