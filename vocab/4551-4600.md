# 필수 영어단어 4551–4600

## 4551. oddly

- 뜻: 이상하게, 기묘하게
- 예문: She looked at me oddly after I told that story.

## 4552. delegation

- 뜻: 대표단, 사절단; 위임
- 예문: A small delegation from our school visited the museum today.

## 4553. revision

- 뜻: 수정, 개정; 복습
- 예문: She spent the weekend on revision for her history exam.

## 4554. betting

- 뜻: 내기, 도박
- 예문: Betting on sports is popular among some of my friends.

## 4555. coldly

- 뜻: 차갑게, 냉정하게
- 예문: He coldly refused to answer any of her questions.

## 4556. irritated

- 뜻: 짜증 난, 화가 난
- 예문: She felt irritated when the phone rang during dinner.

## 4557. sincerely

- 뜻: 진심으로, 성실히
- 예문: I sincerely hope that you enjoy your new job.

## 4558. disapproving

- 뜻: 못마땅해하는, 반대하는
- 예문: His disapproving look made everyone in the room quiet.

## 4559. alternatively

- 뜻: 그 대신에, 또는
- 예문: You can take the bus, or alternatively walk to work.

## 4560. awfully

- 뜻: 몹시, 지독하게
- 예문: The weather was awfully cold during our weekend trip.

## 4561. knit

- 뜻: 뜨개질하다; (골이) 아물다
- 예문: My grandmother likes to knit scarves in the winter.

## 4562. entertaining

- 뜻: 재미있는, 즐거운
- 예문: The movie was entertaining from start to finish.

## 4563. knot

- 뜻: 매듭; 매듭을 짓다
- 예문: He tied a tight knot in the rope before climbing.

## 4564. bitterly

- 뜻: 몹시, 비통하게; 쓰라리게
- 예문: She bitterly regretted not taking the earlier train.

## 4565. mistaken

- 뜻: 잘못된, 오해한
- 예문: I was mistaken about the time of the meeting.

## 4566. entertainer

- 뜻: 연예인, 예능인
- 예문: The entertainer made the children laugh throughout the party.

## 4567. finely

- 뜻: 곱게, 정교하게
- 예문: Chop the onions finely before you add them to the pan.

## 4568. gallon

- 뜻: 갤런 (용량 단위)
- 예문: We bought a gallon of milk at the grocery store.

## 4569. stripe

- 뜻: 줄무늬
- 예문: The cat has a white stripe down its back.

## 4570. curb

- 뜻: 억제하다; 연석
- 예문: Officials hope the new tax will curb excessive spending.

## 4571. photocopy

- 뜻: 복사본; 복사하다
- 예문: Please make a photocopy of this form for your records.

## 4572. knitting

- 뜻: 뜨개질
- 예문: She enjoys knitting while she watches television at night.

## 4573. eleventh

- 뜻: 열한 번째의

## 4574. niece

- 뜻: 조카딸
- 예문: My niece is coming to stay with us this weekend.

## 4575. ninth

- 뜻: 아홉 번째의
- 예문: September is the ninth month of the year.

## 4576. lad

- 뜻: 소년, 젊은이
- 예문: The young lad helped his father carry the boxes.

## 4577. midday

- 뜻: 정오, 한낮
- 예문: We usually eat lunch around midday during the week.

## 4578. twisted

- 뜻: 비틀린, 뒤틀린
- 예문: The twisted metal was all that remained of the bike.

## 4579. unsteady

- 뜻: 불안정한, 흔들리는
- 예문: Her steps were unsteady after the long hike.

## 4580. bandage

- 뜻: 붕대; 붕대를 감다
- 예문: The nurse wrapped a bandage around his injured wrist.

## 4581. rudely

- 뜻: 무례하게
- 예문: He rudely interrupted her while she was speaking.

## 4582. unload

- 뜻: 짐을 내리다, 하역하다

## 4583. thickly

- 뜻: 두껍게, 빽빽하게
- 예문: Snow fell thickly throughout the night over the town.

## 4584. faithful

- 뜻: 충실한, 믿음직한
- 예문: The dog remained faithful to its owner for years.

## 4585. faithfully

- 뜻: 충실히, 성실히
- 예문: She faithfully practiced the piano every single morning.

## 4586. summarize

- 뜻: 요약하다
- 예문: Please summarize the article in two short paragraphs.

## 4587. organizer

- 뜻: 주최자, 기획자; 정리함

## 4588. specialise

- 뜻: 전문으로 하다, 전공하다

## 4589. translator

- 뜻: 번역가

## 4590. marked

- 뜻: 뚜렷한, 현저한; 표시된

## 4591. germ

## 4593. nickname
