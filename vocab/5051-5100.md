# 필수 영어단어 5051–5100

## 5051. duration

- 뜻: 기간, 지속 시간
- 예문: The duration of the movie is just over two hours.

## 5052. respective

- 뜻: 각각의, 각자의
- 예문: The students returned to their respective classrooms after lunch.

## 5053. firework

- 뜻: 폭죽, 불꽃놀이
- 예문: We watched the firework display from the hill above town.

## 5054. enquire

- 뜻: 문의하다, 묻다
- 예문: Please enquire at the front desk about tomorrow's tour times.

## 5055. nervously

- 뜻: 초조하게, 긴장하여
- 예문: She nervously waited for the interview results all afternoon.

## 5056. superb

- 뜻: 훌륭한, 뛰어난
- 예문: The chef prepared a superb meal for our anniversary dinner.

## 5057. aluminium

- 뜻: 알루미늄
- 예문: The window frames are made of light aluminium rather than wood.

## 5058. empirical

- 뜻: 경험적인, 실증적인
- 예문: The study is based on empirical data from thousands of patients.

## 5059. guerrilla

- 뜻: 게릴라, 유격대
- 예문: The guerrilla fighters hid in the mountains for several months.

## 5060. forthcoming

- 뜻: 곧 있을, 다가오는; 선뜻 말하는
- 예문: Details of the forthcoming concert will be posted next week.

## 5061. fifteenth

- 뜻: 열다섯 번째의, 15일의
- 예문: Her fifteenth birthday party was held in the backyard garden.

## 5062. marathon

- 뜻: 마라톤; 장시간 행사
- 예문: He trained for months before running his first marathon last spring.

## 5063. assertion

- 뜻: 주장, 단언
- 예문: Her assertion that the plan would fail proved completely wrong.

## 5064. asylum

- 뜻: 망명; 보호소
- 예문: Thousands of people sought asylum after the war began.

## 5065. referee

- 뜻: 심판; 추천인
- 예문: The referee blew the whistle and stopped the soccer match.

## 5066. sketch

- 뜻: 스케치, 밑그림; 스케치하다
- 예문: She made a quick sketch of the old bridge by the river.

## 5067. prescribe

- 뜻: (약을) 처방하다; 규정하다
- 예문: The doctor will prescribe medicine for your persistent cough.

## 5068. extensively

- 뜻: 광범위하게, 널리
- 예문: He has traveled extensively throughout Europe and East Asia.

## 5069. stabilize

- 뜻: 안정시키다, 안정되다
- 예문: The government tried to stabilize prices after the sudden increase.

## 5070. selective

- 뜻: 선택적인, 선별적인
- 예문: The school is highly selective and accepts only a few applicants.

## 5071. classification

- 뜻: 분류, 구분
- 예문: The library uses a simple classification system for all books.

## 5072. consultation

- 뜻: 상담, 협의
- 예문: She scheduled a consultation with her doctor for next Tuesday.

## 5073. correction

- 뜻: 정정, 교정
- 예문: The teacher made a small correction on my essay draft.

## 5074. congratulate

- 뜻: 축하하다
- 예문: I called to congratulate her on winning the writing prize.

## 5075. idiot

- 뜻: 바보, 멍청이
- 예문: He felt like an idiot after forgetting his own house keys.

## 5076. enthusiastically

- 뜻: 열정적으로, 열렬히
- 예문: The crowd enthusiastically cheered when the band came on stage.

## 5077. predecessor

- 뜻: 전임자, 선행자
- 예문: The new manager changed several policies set by her predecessor.

## 5078. preside

- 뜻: 주재하다, 사회를 보다
- 예문: The judge will preside over the trial beginning next Monday.

## 5079. astonishing

- 뜻: 놀라운, 경이로운
- 예문: The magician performed an astonishing trick with a simple coin.

## 5080. cheerfully

- 뜻: 기분 좋게, 쾌활하게
- 예문: She cheerfully greeted every customer who entered the shop.

## 5081. registration

- 뜻: 등록, 신청
- 예문: Online registration for the course closes at midnight tonight.

## 5082. choir

- 뜻: 합창단
- 예문: She has sung in the school choir since the sixth grade.

## 5083. canal

- 뜻: 운하, 수로
- 예문: Boats moved slowly along the canal through the quiet countryside.

## 5084. oblige

- 뜻: 의무를 지우다; 부탁을 들어주다
- 예문: The new law will oblige shops to close by ten.

## 5085. proposition

- 뜻: 제안, 명제
- 예문: The company made a tempting proposition to buy our small shop.

## 5086. succession

- 뜻: 연속; 계승
- 예문: A succession of storms delayed our flight for several hours.

## 5087. successive

- 뜻: 연속적인, 잇따른
- 예문: He missed three successive meetings because of a family emergency.

## 5088. propaganda

- 뜻: 선전, 선전 활동
- 예문: The government used propaganda to influence public opinion during wartime.

## 5089. noisily

- 뜻: 시끄럽게
- 예문: The children ran noisily down the hallway after the last class.

## 5090. amend

- 뜻: 수정하다, 개정하다
- 예문: The committee voted to amend the rules before the next meeting.

## 5091. landlord

- 뜻: 집주인, 임대인
- 예문: Our landlord promised to fix the leaking roof this weekend.

## 5092. restraint

- 뜻: 자제, 억제; 구속
- 예문: He showed great restraint and did not shout during the argument.

## 5093. surgical

- 뜻: 외과의, 수술의
- 예문: The patient needed surgical treatment after the serious car accident.

## 5094. beloved

- 뜻: 사랑하는, 소중한
- 예문: She kept a photo of her beloved grandmother on the desk.

## 5095. suspension

- 뜻: 정지, 중단; 정학
- 예문: The player received a two-game suspension for fighting on the field.

## 5096. badge

- 뜻: 배지, 휘장
- 예문: Every employee must wear a badge while inside the building.

## 5097. supervise

- 뜻: 감독하다, 관리하다
- 예문: A teacher will supervise the students during the field trip.

## 5098. allowance

- 뜻: 용돈, 수당; 허용
- 예문: Her parents give her a weekly allowance for snacks and books.

## 5099. stiffly

- 뜻: 뻣뻣하게, 경직되게
- 예문: He walked stiffly after sitting in the same chair all day.

## 5100. copyright

- 뜻: 저작권
- 예문: The author holds the copyright to every story in the book.
