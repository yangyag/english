# 필수 영어단어 801–850

## 801. them

- 뜻: 그들, 그것들을
- 예문: I saw them at the park after school yesterday.

## 802. me

- 뜻: 나, 나를
- 예문: Can you help me with this heavy box?

## 803. first

- 뜻: 첫 번째의, 먼저
- 예문: She was the first person to arrive at work.

## 804. also

- 뜻: 또한, 역시
- 예문: He also brought some snacks for the meeting.

## 805. something

- 뜻: 무언가, 어떤 것
- 예문: I heard something strange in the kitchen last night.

## 806. mean

- 뜻: 의미하다, 뜻하다
- 예문: What does this word mean in simple English?

## 807. two

- 뜻: 둘, 두 개의
- 예문: She bought two tickets for the concert tonight.

## 808. life

- 뜻: 삶, 인생
- 예문: He wants a quiet life in a small town.

## 809. really

- 뜻: 정말, 실제로
- 예문: I really enjoyed the movie we watched last night.

## 810. programme

- 뜻: 프로그램, 계획
- 예문: This TV programme is popular with young people.

## 811. like

- 뜻: ~을 좋아하다; ~처럼
- 예문: I like coffee with a little milk in the morning.

## 812. would

- 뜻: ~할 것이다, ~하곤 했다
- 예문: I would help you if I had more time.

## 813. another

- 뜻: 또 하나의, 다른
- 예문: Would you like another cup of tea after dinner?

## 814. own

- 뜻: 자신의; 소유하다
- 예문: She wants to start her own small business next year.

## 815. still

- 뜻: 아직도, 여전히
- 예문: He is still waiting for a reply from the company.

## 816. different

- 뜻: 다른, 차이가 있는
- 예문: These two plans have very different costs and risks.

## 817. seem

- 뜻: ~처럼 보이다, ~인 것 같다
- 예문: You seem tired after such a long day at work.

## 818. more

- 뜻: 더 많은, 더욱
- 예문: We need more time to finish this project today.

## 819. part

- 뜻: 부분, 역할
- 예문: This is the most important part of the whole plan.

## 820. company

- 뜻: 회사, 함께함
- 예문: She works for a large company in the city center.

## 821. most

- 뜻: 대부분의, 가장
- 예문: Most students in this class enjoy reading after school.

## 822. such

- 뜻: 그러한, 그런
- 예문: I have never seen such a beautiful garden before.

## 823. without

- 뜻: ~없이
- 예문: He left the house without his keys this morning.

## 824. important

- 뜻: 중요한
- 예문: It is important to drink enough water every day.

## 825. later

- 뜻: 나중에, 더 늦은
- 예문: I will call you later when I finish work.

## 826. believe

- 뜻: 믿다, 생각하다
- 예문: I believe we can finish this task before Friday.

## 827. while

- 뜻: ~하는 동안; 그런데
- 예문: Please wait here while I find your order.

## 828. social

- 뜻: 사회의, 사교의
- 예문: She enjoys social events with friends on the weekend.

## 829. each

- 뜻: 각각의, 각자
- 예문: Each student must bring a notebook to class tomorrow.

## 830. three

- 뜻: 셋, 세 개의
- 예문: We waited three hours at the station for the train.

## 831. person

- 뜻: 사람
- 예문: She is the only person who knows the password.

## 832. someone

- 뜻: 누군가
- 예문: Someone left a message for you at the front desk.

## 833. actually

- 뜻: 사실은, 실제로
- 예문: I actually forgot to send the email this morning.

## 834. system

- 뜻: 시스템, 체계
- 예문: The new computer system is faster than the old one.

## 835. business

- 뜻: 사업, 일
- 예문: He started a small business selling coffee near campus.

## 836. human

- 뜻: 인간의, 사람
- 예문: Every human needs food, water, and rest to live.

## 837. health

- 뜻: 건강
- 예문: Regular exercise is good for your health and mood.

## 838. though

- 뜻: ~이지만, 그러나
- 예문: The walk was long, though the weather stayed warm.

## 839. maybe

- 뜻: 아마, 어쩌면
- 예문: Maybe we can meet for lunch after the meeting.

## 840. create

- 뜻: 만들다, 창조하다
- 예문: They want to create a new app for language learners.

## 841. reason

- 뜻: 이유, 이성
- 예문: The main reason for the delay was heavy rain.

## 842. state

- 뜻: 상태, 주(州); 말하다
- 예문: The old house is in a bad state after the storm.

## 843. government

- 뜻: 정부
- 예문: The government announced a new plan to support schools.

## 844. whether

- 뜻: ~인지 아닌지
- 예문: I am not sure whether she will come to dinner.

## 845. information

- 뜻: 정보
- 예문: Please send me more information about the training course.

## 846. experience

- 뜻: 경험, 경험하다
- 예문: She has a lot of experience in teaching young children.

## 847. issue

- 뜻: 문제, 쟁점
- 예문: The team discussed the main issue during the morning meeting.

## 848. ever

- 뜻: 지금까지, 언젠가
- 예문: Have you ever visited this city during the winter?

## 849. area

- 뜻: 지역, 영역
- 예문: This area of the city is quiet at night.

## 850. power

- 뜻: 힘, 권력, 전력
- 예문: The storm cut the power in our neighborhood last night.
